## Q21. NSW second-hand electrical: test/tag or label before resale? ⛔ (blocked online)

- Every route to the regulation text returned **HTTP 403 to automated access**: legislation.nsw.gov.au (HTML, PDF and XML exports), AustLII, and the NSW Parliament bill files.
- Search engines didn't surface any clause in the **Gas and Electricity (Consumer Safety) Regulation 2018** that specifically imposes a test/tag or "untested" label duty on second-hand sellers. Part 6 ("general restrictions on sale or other disposal") covers warnings and non-conforming articles.
- The only 2026 change found, **sl-2026-301 (Compliance Certificates) Amendment Regulation 2026**, deals with **electrical installation work certificates**, not resale of appliances. [Secondary — search summary]
- Explicit second-hand label regimes were found for **Tasmania** (CBOS guide) and **Queensland** (WorkSafe Qld "selling second-hand" page). Both were also bot-blocked, but their titles and snippets confirm the regimes exist. NSW wasn't shown to have an equivalent.
- **[Derived] Status:** no NSW blanket test-and-tag-before-resale rule could be found or confirmed. The NSW Government's own page only requires that the article was approved when new, and that sellers keep buyers safe.
- **To close it (5 minutes in a normal browser):** open https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/sl-2018-0501 → Ctrl-F "second-hand" / "secondhand" and read Part 6. If nothing is there, the interim policy in `docs/claudegaps-research/05 §5.3` stands (test mains items after a test-and-tag course, or don't sell them).

## Q29. Starting a Repair Café ✅
- **Repair Café International** offers a **digital starter kit for a voluntary one-off fee of €49**: manual (venue, fixers, tools, publicity, funding), logo, poster/flyer templates, **liability/registration forms**, RepairMonitor data logging, and a listing on the global map (1,200+ cafés, 30+ countries). [Primary] https://www.repaircafe.org/en/join/start-your-own/ ; https://www.repaircafe.org/en/faq/
- **Insurance:** public liability is recommended, typically **through the host organisation or venue** (e.g. a council library, Men's Shed or community centre). [Primary]
- **AU examples to copy:** St Kilda Repair Café FAQ (https://www.stkildarepaircafe.org.au/faqs/); Repair It Lake Mac (council-linked, NSW; podcast in `docs/claudegaps-research/07`).
- Nearest active café to Dubbo: **none found in the Central West** (see 05).
- **[Derived] Low-cost plan:** host at the Dubbo Community Men's Shed or the Macquarie Regional Library under the venue's insurance. Run monthly; electronics fixing table only to start, with the lithium and data rules from the intake policy. Dubbo Regional Council's waste-education budget is a possible sponsor. **[Open — CALL]** the venue and Council.
