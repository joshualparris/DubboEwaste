# Intake policy (draft)

**Model:** high-quality, sellable electronics only. Business/school clients by appointment (Tue/Sat). Public walk-ins only if council and Fair Trading say it's OK.

## Accept
- Business laptops about 5-6 years old or newer with decent specs, desktops that take an SSD upgrade, decent monitors, tablets, current-ish phones.
- Working flat-screen/smart TVs under an agreed size/age limit.
- Gear **unenrolled and unlocked** (no MDM/Intune/Autopilot, Apple Activation Lock, Google FRP, BIOS/firmware passwords).
- Gear with a signed ownership/transfer statement.

## Refuse
- CRT TVs and monitors, projection TVs, printers, anything old and low-value.
- Loose or swollen/damaged lithium batteries; e-bike/e-scooter batteries.
- Gear without ownership paperwork; anything that looks stolen.
- Locked or enrolled devices.

## Exit for items that fail the profit test
1. Refuse at intake (best). 2. Small intake fee. 3. Australian Metal Recycling for accepted zero-value e-waste/metal, or NTCRS/Whylandra where legally available. AMR currently says e-waste is free but lithium batteries are not accepted; confirm exact Dubbo categories and commercial terms before the pilot.

## Wipe and test
- Standard: NIST SP 800-88 style sanitisation; certificate per device.
- HDDs: overwrite (nwipe). **SSD/NVMe: secure erase / crypto-erase, not a simple overwrite.** Phones: factory reset plus account/lock removal.
- Record serial, IMEI/MAC where applicable.
- If a dealer licence applies, check how the 14-day no-alteration hold interacts with wiping.

## Safety
- Metal container for damaged cells, concrete floor, away from the house and family areas.
- No unattended charging; smoke alarm and extinguisher in the shed.
- No stockpiling; move leftovers within a week or two.
- Keep visitors out of the work area. A sole trader is still a PCBU under WHS (SafeWork NSW).

## Records
- Client, item, serial/IMEI, ownership statement, wipe certificate, sale price, buyer details where required.
