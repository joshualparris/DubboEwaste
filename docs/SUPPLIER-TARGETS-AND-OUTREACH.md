# Supplier Targets and Outreach

## Priority tiers

### Tier 1 — repeat fleet sources
- local MSPs / IT providers;
- independent schools and school systems;
- accounting/legal/professional offices;
- health/allied-health practices;
- larger trades/construction businesses;
- NFPs/community organisations.

### Tier 2 — institutional/referral
- councils/government offices where procurement allows;
- charities with donated technology;
- repair shops with uneconomic-but-reusable stock;
- businesses undergoing Windows 10 refreshes.

### Tier 3 — public
Delay broad public intake until planning/licensing/insurance gates are closed.

## Discovery fields
For every target:
- organisation;
- contact/role;
- approximate device count;
- refresh frequency;
- current disposal route;
- data/security requirements;
- whether devices are released from MDM/Autopilot/ABM;
- pickup expectations;
- reporting/certificate needs;
- reuse/social-impact interest;
- next action.

## MSP outreach pack

**Position:** Dubbo-based reuse-first outlet for retired client hardware, not a competitor for their support contracts.

Core message:
- local collection by arrangement;
- serialised intake;
- secure handling of data-bearing devices;
- reuse/refurbish before material recycling;
- clear rejection criteria;
- downstream documentation;
- no claim of certified destruction unless service used supports it.

Three questions:
1. What do you currently do with retired client laptops/desktops/phones?
2. Roughly how many units leave your clients each month/quarter?
3. What evidence would you need from a local reuse/refurb partner: serial report, wipe report, destruction/recycling certificate, value return?

**Conflict gate:** do not approach MSPs until the operator's employment/conflict position is resolved.

## School outreach pack
Ask:
- typical refresh cycle and device families;
- ownership/lease restrictions;
- who authorises disposal;
- MDM/Autopilot/Google/Apple release process;
- data-erasure requirements;
- whether reuse/donation is permitted;
- whether serialised disposition reporting is required.

Offer:
- scheduled collection;
- chain-of-custody;
- reuse-first triage;
- documented erase/disposition;
- return/donation pathway where approved;
- downstream recycling for non-viable assets.

## Charity/community outreach
Goal: match usable devices to real need without creating unsupported dumping.

Ask:
- required device specs;
- ability to support devices;
- Windows/Chrome/macOS preference;
- quantity;
- recipient eligibility/privacy;
- whether chargers/accessories are required;
- reporting expectations.

## One-page service promise
DubboEwaste aims to:
1. know who supplied each item;
2. track each serial/IMEI;
3. secure unwiped equipment;
4. clear locks legitimately;
5. sanitise and verify data-bearing media;
6. maximise reuse where safe/economic;
7. disclose condition honestly;
8. record downstream recycling;
9. not accept items with no safe/legal route.
