# Greenbox, EraseIT, Sims Lifecycle Services and Renew IT: size and Dubbo coverage

Research checked **2 October 2026**. This report answers the four-company comparison and records what their advertised processes do with retired devices. It complements the [major-player market map](AUSTRALIAN-EWASTE-ITAD-MAJOR-PLAYERS.md), [Dubbo disposal prospects](DUBBO-DEVICE-DISPOSAL-PROSPECTS.md) and [local independent-operator investigation](DUBBO-INDEPENDENT-DEVICE-RECOVERY-OPERATORS.md).

## Findings

These are substantial operators, but **Greenbox and EraseIT now belong to the same group**: Greenbox's acquisition was announced in 2025. Counting them as four independent competing groups would overstate the field. [N03–N04; EVD-ITAD-003]

**None of the four lists a Dubbo facility in the location sources reviewed.** Greenbox advertises national service capacity, and Renew IT explicitly advertises collections anywhere in Australia. Both include Dubbo geographically in their advertised scope; a particular pickup, price or turnaround in postcode 2830 still needs a quote. For EraseIT and SLS, their networks make a Dubbo job plausible, but no named Dubbo customer, regular local route or binding service commitment was established. This distinction matters when assessing actual competition. [N01–N02, N06, N08, N11–N12; EVD-ITAD-008–009, 015]

## Corrections to the initial descriptions

- **EraseIT's historical “first” claim is specific:** first Australian-owned ITAD provider to join the secure-destruction association NAID. Membership, certification and inventing a certification are different claims. The linked announcement supports the membership claim. [N18; EVD-ITAD-014]
- **Greenbox explicitly says its four Australian facilities are DISP-approved.** This is stronger than a generic military-grade slogan, but it remains a company statement here: current accreditation levels/site scope and approval for a particular defence contract were not independently checked. [N01, N05; EVD-ITAD-016]
- **EraseIT's ownership is current; older location text is inconsistent.** Its about page retains Tullamarine text, while current contact details give Campbellfield care of Greenbox. Use the current contact for routing and confirm the operating facility for a job. [N04, N06]
- **Renew's annual asset figure is a partner claim**, not a reported financial-year result. Do not turn it into a device count for Dubbo or compare it directly with SLS's component-inclusive unit metric. [N07, N13]

## Implications for the Dubbo project

**Interpretation:** the nationals are credible competitors for centrally procured enterprise, government and data-centre work. That does not establish that they currently win any of the specific [14 local prospect accounts](DUBBO-DEVICE-DISPOSAL-PROSPECTS.md).

A local collector could investigate a logistics/referral relationship as well as direct competition. Greenbox advertises partner arrangements and SLS describes an international subcontractor network. Neither is proof of an available Dubbo appointment. [N08, N19]

The useful qualification questions are: will you collect in postcode 2830; what equipment and minimum batch qualify; who transports it and under whose custody records; which facility receives it; what data work/reporting is included; how is resale value shared; and can a local business aggregate or refer loads? These are proposed questions, not an agreed partnership or prices. No companies were contacted for this research. This report changes no launch-gate status in [CURRENT-STATE.md](CURRENT-STATE.md).
