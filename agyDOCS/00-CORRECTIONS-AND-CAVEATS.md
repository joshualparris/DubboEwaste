# Corrections and Caveats

This document serves as a formal correction to the initial deep-research documents generated in this folder. The initial research contained several oversimplifications, unverified generic claims presented as company-specific facts, and lacked strict primary-source citations. 

Before relying on the operational guides in this repository, please note the following verified corrections:

## 1. Competitor Operations (Fliptech & Sircel)
* **Correction:** The `ITAD-Operations-Blueprint.md` describes *generic* enterprise ITAD best practices (like Makor ERP, Blancco PXE wiping, and A/B/C/D grading). It was originally prompted to describe how *specific* companies like Fliptech and Sircel operate.
* **Caveat:** There is no public, verified evidence confirming the exact internal software stack or grading rubrics used specifically by Fliptech (Warriewood) or Sircel (Sydney). The blueprint should be treated as an industry standard to aspire to, not a factual reflection of those competitors' internal floors.

## 2. Platform Economics & eBay Fees
* **Correction:** The eBay fee-free tier for sellers under $25,000 is not simply a "private vs. business" distinction.
* **Caveat:** The $25,000 threshold includes taxes and shipping. An electronics refurbishing business will likely cross this threshold rapidly. Once crossed, sellers are moved to a Pro Starter plan which *does* charge final value fees. Furthermore, sellers on the free tier are mandated to buy postage labels through eBay.

## 3. Shipping & Australia Post Lithium Regulations
* **Correction:** The `NSW-Market-Sales-Channels.md` stated that loose batteries or those packed alongside a device simply need a "Road Transport Only" label.
* **Caveat:** This is dangerously outdated. Current Australia Post regulations state that lithium batteries must **not** be packed by themselves or alongside a device under standard domestic postal services. Always refer directly to the live [AusPost Dangerous Goods Guide](https://auspost.com.au/sending/check-sending-guidelines/dangerous-prohibited-items) before shipping.

## 4. Educational Resources & TAFE
* **Correction:** The TAFE NSW UEE30920 Certificate III in Electronics and Communications was listed as a learning resource.
* **Caveat:** This is a ~3-year, on-campus qualification that generally requires relevant employment or an apprenticeship. It is a formal trade pathway, not a quick, practical course for someone looking to start flipping immediately.

## 5. Technical Thresholds (Windows 11)
* **Correction:** `Triage-Skills-Guide.md` stated 8GB RAM is the "minimum" for Windows 11.
* **Caveat:** The *official* Microsoft minimum is 4GB RAM and a TPM 2.0 module. The 8GB recommendation is a practical business choice for refurbishers to ensure usable performance, not a hard software restriction.

## 6. Australian Consumer Law (ACL)
* **Correction:** Describing a "Sold As Is" sign as strictly "illegal" in NSW is an overstatement.
* **Caveat:** The ACL is national law, not NSW-specific. While a blanket "sold as is" policy cannot override consumer guarantees for businesses, the "acceptable quality" guarantee does *not* apply to specific defects if the seller explicitly points them out to the buyer before the sale (preferably in writing, per NSW Fair Trading). 

## 7. Local Sourcing (Substation33)
* **Correction:** Substation33 was cited as operating in Meadowbrook.
* **Caveat:** Historical sources (2019-2020) place them in Kingston, Logan. Their current operational status and exact location for accepting e-waste must be verified by direct contact before relying on them as a partner.
