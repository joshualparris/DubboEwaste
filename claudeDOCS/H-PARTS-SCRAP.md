## 50. Which other NSW board/CPU/RAM scrap buyers publish prices?

**Status: 🟡 Partly answered.**

The strongest additional NSW buyer found is **Specialised Recycling**, a Sydney industrial e-waste/PCB buyer. It accepts:
- mixed PCBs of low/medium/high grade;
- laptops and desktop components;
- server/networking gear;
- commercial e-waste.

It publishes a **500 kg minimum for free scheduled pickup** and same-week EFT settlement. Instead of a simple grade-by-grade price sheet, it publishes a commodity-linked valuation model using LME copper and precious-metal benchmarks.

Current commercial benchmark inputs displayed when checked:
- Copper: **$17.70/kg**
- Gold: **$216.29/g**
- Silver: **$3.38/g**

Source:
- https://www.specialisedrecycling.com.au/

I did **not** find another NSW buyer with a granular public CPU/RAM/PCB payout sheet comparable to The Board Guy's.

**DubboEwaste takeaway:**  
- small/irregular loads → The Board Guy is presently much more transparent and accessible;
- larger commercial PCB loads → Specialised Recycling becomes relevant, especially around the 500 kg pickup threshold;
- keep researching buyers as volumes grow, because wholesale negotiated rates can exceed public small-lot rates.

## 51. Does Shred-X publish regional collection or mail-in terms for drive destruction?

**Status: 🟡 Partly answered.**

Shred-X explicitly says it provides secure destruction throughout **metropolitan and regional NSW**. Its NSW footprint includes Wetherill Park, Warragamba and Newcastle, and its services include:
- hard-drive degaussing, sanitisation and destruction;
- on-site hard-drive/media destruction;
- facility-based destruction;
- e-waste management and IT value recovery.

Its Wetherill Park facility is currently NAID AAA certified through **31 Dec 2026** for physical hard-drive and solid-state-device destruction, among other services.

Primary sources:
- https://www.shred-x.com.au/location/new-south-wales/
- https://www.shred-x.com.au/location/warragamba/
- https://www.shred-x.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NAID-AAA-Certificates-Shred-X-2026-merged.pdf

A Shred-X NSW operational document also describes collection from clients around Sydney and regional NSW using company trucks.

What I **did not find**:
- a published post/mail-in-a-drive program;
- a published Dubbo-specific collection minimum;
- a public per-drive or regional call-out price.

The public service model appears to be **quote-based collection, on-site destruction or facility processing**, rather than a clearly advertised consumer mail-in service.

**DubboEwaste takeaway:** Shred-X is a credible downstream option for certified destruction, but Dubbo pricing/logistics still needs a quote. Do not assume drives can simply be posted to Wetherill Park without written acceptance.

# Batch conclusions

1. The Board Guy now gives DubboEwaste a concrete NSW scrap-floor schedule for boards, RAM, CPUs, drives and whole units.
2. The Board Guy's lack of minimum quantity and mail-in option is unusually well matched to a small Dubbo pilot.
3. Specialised Recycling is more relevant once commercial PCB volumes become large; its free-pickup threshold is 500 kg.
4. Shred-X clearly services regional NSW and is certified for drive destruction, but mail-in terms and Dubbo pricing are not publicly stated.
