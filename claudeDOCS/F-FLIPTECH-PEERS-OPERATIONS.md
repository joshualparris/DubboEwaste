## 36. What are WorkVentures' grade definitions?

**Status: 🟡 Partly answered — no public A/B/C grading table found.**

WorkVentures publicly describes the cosmetic quality of the refurbished devices it sells as generally **"good to very good"**. It says devices may have scratches or marks externally, that internal cosmetic quality is typically very good, and that systems are checked for damage or bad scratches. Its refurbishment page describes an end-to-end process of secure collection, data destruction, assessment/triage, recycling or reuse, and reporting.

What I could **not** find is a WorkVentures public A/B/C/D or equivalent grade matrix. Do not invent one or map another seller's grading system onto WorkVentures.

Useful operational detail:
- triage/testing/repairs are performed;
- data can be securely erased;
- equipment is cleaned and boxed;
- reuse-vs-recycle is decided at assessment;
- MDM locks are explicitly identified as a barrier to reuse.

Primary sources:
- https://shop.workventures.com.au/faq/
- https://workventures.com.au/refurbishment/

**DubboEwaste takeaway:** WorkVentures supports the idea of a practical cosmetic/functional assessment, but its public material does not justify copying a named A/B/C grading standard.

The site says it records condition and value during asset audit, and describes its resale stock as "wholesale-grade refurbished IT equipment", but I found **no public incoming A/B/C purchase-grade table** and no published buy-price schedule.

Primary source:
- https://www.linkbytes.com.au/

**DubboEwaste takeaway:** LinkBytes is a useful benchmark for fleet buyback and shows that a ~10-device enquiry is commercially meaningful, but its intake grading and price floors remain non-public.

## 40. How do Free Geek and the Restart Project document their refurb processes?

**Status: ✅ Answered.**

### Free Geek
Free Geek publishes unusually detailed process documentation.

Its broad flow:
1. donation/intake;
2. data-bearing devices go to a secure data area;
3. reusable drives are wiped; failed/non-reusable storage is physically destroyed or sent to vetted downstream vendors;
4. devices are assessed for **refurbish vs recycle**;
5. approximately 30% of received material is described as moving to reuse, with the remainder moving toward recycling;
6. refurbished PCs/Macs receive OS installation plus structured testing;
7. devices go to sale/community programs.

Published PC tests include:
- CPU stress;
- drive test;
- speakers/audio;
- Ethernet/Wi-Fi;
- screen colour/touch;
- mic/webcam;
- keyboard/trackpad;
- USB;
- RAM tests where applicable.

Named tools include iPXE, stress-ng, MemTest86, HD Sentinel, DriveDx, Geekbench, PerformanceTest and FurMark.

Free Geek says its program target for many community laptops is **50%+ original battery capacity** and at least one hour indicated runtime; sale stock may differ.

Its FY25 report also says it has developed open-source software that can erase data, install operating systems, test hardware and create sales listings.

Sources:
- https://www.freegeek.org/about/our-process
- https://www.freegeek.org/our-refurb-process
- https://www.freegeek.org/take-action-donate-technology/data-security
- https://www.freegeek.org/sites/default/files/2026-01/FG%20Impact%20Report%20FY25_V6.pdf

### Restart Project
Restart is primarily a **community repair** model rather than an ITAD/refurb factory. At Restart Parties, volunteer repairers work with device owners. Devices are recorded as:
- repaired;
- repairable/follow-up needed;
- or end-of-life when fixer and owner decide repair is not realistic or cost-effective.

Restart says around **18% of repairs require spare parts**, and about **6%** of cases leave with owners intending to finish the repair themselves. It encourages reuse before recycling even for end-of-life items and publishes/open-sources repair-data tools.

Sources:
- https://therestartproject.org/
- https://therestartproject.org/faq/

**DubboEwaste takeaway:** Free Geek is the stronger template for a repeatable refurb bench/SOP; Restart is the stronger template for community-assisted diagnosis, repair decision-making and repair-data collection.

## 41. What are Back Market Australia's seller-onboarding requirements?

**Status: ✅ Substantially answered from Back Market's own Australian pages.**

Back Market Australia has a current seller recruitment page and says it works with professional refurbishers. Publicly visible requirements/standards include:

- sellers are vetted; Back Market says only about **1 in 3 applicants** is accepted;
- proven industry experience / track record is considered;
- sellers are approved for particular product categories;
- compliance with Back Market's **Quality Charter** is mandatory;
- devices must be **100% functional** irrespective of cosmetic grade;
- devices must be factory reset/data scrubbed and fully cleaned;
- no locked, stolen or fraudulently sourced devices;
- standard battery health is at least **80% capacity**; Premium grade is higher;
- Australian purchases include **1-year warranty** and **30-day free returns**;
- sellers are continuously monitored through performance data, mystery shopping, reviews and on-site/technical quality checks;
- grades used in Australia are Fair, Good, Excellent and Premium.

What is **not** exposed on the public onboarding page: the seller commission schedule, any minimum stock/volume requirement, required company age/revenue, and the complete contractual Quality Charter.

Sources:
- https://www.backmarket.com.au/en-au/seller/home
- https://www.backmarket.com.au/en-au/quality
- https://help.backmarket.com/hc/en-au/articles/360013900119-Is-Back-Market-legit
- https://help.backmarket.com/hc/en-au/articles/360026656634-What-condition-will-my-device-be-in

## 42. Does Reebelo's primary seller-terms text confirm the second-hand dealer registration requirement?

**Status: ✅ Yes.**

Reebelo Australia's Terms of Service explicitly state that a prospective seller, by applying and signing up, warrants that it has:

> a valid registration as a second hand dealer

and all relevant approvals needed for trading telecommunications equipment, plus any other legally required registrations/processes.

Seller registration is not automatic: applicants contact Reebelo and the seller account is created only after Reebelo validates/accepts it. Reebelo also describes sellers as professional refurbishers/certified sellers subject to vetting.

## 43. What would it cost and take to host Snipe-IT for a one-person business?

**Status: ✅ Answered.**

Snipe-IT offers two realistic paths:

### Self-host
- software licence: **free / open source**;
- unlimited users/assets from Snipe-IT's side;
- requires a web server and ongoing responsibility for installation, backups, upgrades, security and maintenance;
- can run on Linux, macOS or Windows; an official Docker image is available.

For a one-person operation already comfortable administering Linux/Docker, this is the lowest-cash-cost option. Actual VPS/domain/backup cost depends on the host and is not a Snipe-IT fee.

### Official hosted service
Current official pricing, USD:
- Basic: **US$39.99/month** or **US$399.99/year**
- Small Business: **US$99.99/month** or **US$999.99/year**
- Dedicated small: **US$249.99/month** or **US$2,499.99/year**

Snipe-IT says hosted accounts can be provisioned in a **Sydney** AWS region.

Sources:
- https://snipeitapp.com/pricing
- https://snipeitapp.com/faq
- https://github.com/grokability/snipe-it

**DubboEwaste takeaway:** self-hosting is financially attractive for a pilot, but Snipe-IT is an IT asset-management system, not a purpose-built ITAD resale/settlement ERP. Use it as an inventory/chain-of-custody starting point, not proof that it covers every downstream ITAD workflow.

## 44. What are the lithium shipping rules for Sendle, CouriersPlease, StarTrack and Aramex?

**Status: ✅🟡 Answered for Sendle/CouriersPlease/Aramex; StarTrack public detail remains incomplete.**

### Sendle
Sendle's Australian support page currently says:
- **small quantities of brand-new electronics with built-in batteries** are accepted domestically under stated conditions;
- small quantities of lithium-ion batteries may be sent domestically because parcels travel by road, with an SDS and battery caution label;
- lithium-ion batteries are not accepted for international Sendle shipments;
- old mobile phones being sent for recycling are listed as not acceptable.

Source:
- https://support.sendle.com/hc/en-au/articles/205204738-What-can-t-I-Sendle

This wording is particularly important for DubboEwaste: **used/refurbished devices should not simply be assumed to meet Sendle's "brand new electronic devices" allowance. Get written confirmation before making Sendle the default refurb shipping carrier.**

### CouriersPlease
CouriersPlease says:
- dangerous goods are only accepted domestically in limited quantities for **pre-approved EDI account customers**;
- no dangerous goods internationally;
- lithium batteries cannot travel by air in its network;
- lithium batteries can travel by **road only** domestically under its DG process;
- UN3480/UN3090 are used for loose lithium batteries and UN3481/UN3491 for batteries packed in/with equipment;
- DG service is unavailable to Tasmania;
- regional DG consignments can require documentation/surcharges.

Sources:
- https://www.couriersplease.com.au/dangerous-goods
- https://support.couriersplease.com.au/hc/en-au/articles/31616595111577-What-are-dangerous-goods
- https://www.couriersplease.com.au/media/Dangerous_Goods_Guide_for_Pre_approved_Customers_228932499a.pdf

### Aramex Australia
Aramex's current conditions say **Dangerous Goods are prohibited** in its standard network. Its domestic conditions require the customer to warrant that tendered goods are not dangerous goods under the Australian Dangerous Goods Code. Its booking flow requires confirmation that the consignment contains no dangerous goods.

Sources:
- https://www.aramex.com.au/contact-us/faqs/joining-aramex/are-there-any-items-you-dont-carry/
- https://www.aramex.com.au/terms-and-conditions/conditions-of-carriage/domestic-conditions-of-carriage/
- https://www.aramex.com.au/tools/fastlabel-product-calculator/

**Practical reading:** do not book lithium-battery electronics through standard Aramex without explicit written carrier confirmation that the particular device/consignment is accepted.

### StarTrack
The current public StarTrack pages retrieved in this pass provide general freight/support information but did **not** expose a clear public lithium-battery acceptance matrix equivalent to CouriersPlease's page. StarTrack is an Australia Post Group company, but Australia Post parcel rules should not automatically be assumed to be identical to a StarTrack business contract.

Public support directs customers to their StarTrack sales representative/contact centre for service-specific questions:
- https://forms.startrack.com.au/support/faqs/

**Action before using StarTrack for refurbished laptops/phones:** obtain the dangerous-goods/lithium terms attached to the exact StarTrack service/account being quoted.

# Batch conclusions

The most useful findings for a Dubbo pilot are:

1. WorkVentures does not provide a public grade matrix to copy; define DubboEwaste's own transparent grading standard.
2. LinkBytes openly invites disposal projects from about 10 devices upward, giving a useful small-fleet competitor benchmark.
3. Fliptech's specific ISO certificate numbers/scopes remain unverified; don't repeat certification claims without the actual certificates/register records.
4. Real ITAD/refurb work is strongly centred on intake security, testing, diagnostics, repair, data handling and dispatch.
5. Free Geek provides a practical, public testing/refurb SOP worth borrowing from.
6. Back Market has a high gate: professional seller vetting, 100% functionality, quality controls, ≥80% standard battery health, warranty and returns.
7. Reebelo explicitly requires valid second-hand-dealer registration where applicable.
8. Snipe-IT can be run free self-hosted and is realistic for a one-person pilot.
9. Lithium shipping differs materially by carrier; there is no safe one-rule-fits-all policy. Carrier/service-specific rules must be checked before booking.
