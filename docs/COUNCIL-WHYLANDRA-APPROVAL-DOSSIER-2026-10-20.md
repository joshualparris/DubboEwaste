# Dubbo Council e-waste reuse pilot — approval and evidence dossier (20 October 2026)

**Status: desk research and proposal preparation, NOT approval.** Prepared against Council's 20 October 2026 decision (held in private correspondence; deliberately not reproduced here). This public document records the substance of the decision without publishing confidential email content or personal/device information.

## Decision and boundaries

Council declined the proposed separate diversion/collection of laptops/desktops from Whylandra while retaining its existing e-waste arrangement. Council identified data sanitisation, privacy, chain of custody, insurance, liability, governance and reputation as decision factors and indicated further assessment, approvals and a written agreement would be required for a change. **No operational collection authorisation, exemption, contract variation or Council endorsement has been given.**

Three distinct decisions must not be confused:
1. **Operator legality:** Whether a small home/workshop refurbisher may receive, store, refurbish, resell or recycle devices under planning, second-hand-dealer, environmental, WHS and consumer law.
2. **Operational assurance:** Whether the operator can demonstrate actual process performance with evidence, qualified service providers, insurance and audit trails.
3. **Council consent / existing contract:** Whether Council can and will authorise removing any collected item from the facility's contracted stream, and whether the current agreement permits it. This can remain **NO** even if (1) and (2) are satisfied.

Proposed new project stages are **research only** until signed site authority, contract permission and applicable approvals are obtained. No scavenging, informal collections, unsupervised tipping-area access or implied Council partnership.

## Suggested Council decision package (in approval order)

### Gate A — preliminary classification (do not seek access yet)
- A1: 2-page scope: legal entity/ABN, source of devices, precise categories (laptops/desktops only), max 5–10 screened devices as an illustrative cap **subject to actual agreement**, no bulk mixed waste, no loose/damaged batteries, no unattended donation, no public scavenging, no promises of Council branding.
- A2: Alternative business models compared: **A** direct private donors bypassing Council; **B** incumbent recycler's authorised subcontract; **C** Council-controlled, segregated, risk-assessed one-off demonstration; **D** later competitive opportunity. State whether the devices are saleable Council assets/waste, whether any cash/benefit is exchanged, and whether Council procurement applies.
- A3: Written answer identifying contracting route, decision-maker/delegation, incumbent consent, legal title, site authority, waste-facility development and environment-licence constraints. Gate A is **open**.

### Gate B — insurance, privacy, chain of custody
- B1: Lawyer-reviewed draft agreement: definitions, site rights, title vesting and risk transfer, rejected devices, Council termination for convenience, safe recall, indemnities and proportionate limitations, insurance conditions, audit rights, records retention, privacy, confidentiality, media, complaints and public communications.
- B2: Insurer's actual underwritten coverage and exclusions for collection, transit, fire/battery, premises, visitors, products/completed operations, faulty repairs, property in custody, cyber/data loss, volunteer/employer liabilities as applicable. Exact limits from Council/insurer, **do not invent $10m/$20m**.
- B3: Demonstrated AssetFlow reconciliation: offer → screening → authorised transfer (person/date/time) → restricted unwiped storage → media assessment → sanitisation/independent verification → repaired/graded device → donation/sale or recycler receipt. Record immutable evidence references; keep personal data out of public repos. Council to agree sample audit rate and certificate fields.
- B4: Breach response with immediate containment, incident notification times agreed by contract, forensic hold, law-enforcement/waste-authority escalation and no unchecked public reports.

### Gate C — physical trial, sign-off and audit
- C1: Written site safety approval and induction; safe traffic separation, PPE, lifting, lithium quarantine/escalation, no live mains repairs, no hazardous loads.
- C2: Signed pilot agreement, approvals, insurance certificates, named supervising officer, authorised opening hours and custody process **before** any Council-device transfer.
- C3: Run limited short trial only if written approval exists, track measured input/output, near misses, incident response, environmental/economic impact and unresolved units. Stop authority rests with both parties.
- C4: Independent audit and close-out report; reconcile every serial/asset; no Council publicity without consent; agree renewal, amendment or exit.

## Precise questions for Council (do not assume their answer)

1. Is any pilot categorised as procurement, waste-service contract variation, community partnership, lease/licence, disposal of Council-controlled property or something else?
2. What Council position and delegation can authorise a one-off, no-cost pilot? What approvals (officer/executive/Council meeting, procurement/legal, contractor, EPA/planning) would be necessary?
3. Who is the present legally contracted e-waste service provider, for what streams/sites, and are reuse diversion and authorised subcontracting possible? May Council disclose redacted relevant clauses or provide scope guidance?
4. At which step do deposited items become the Council's or contractor's property; can resident authorisation occur before deposition?
5. What exact risk matrix, assurance standards, pilot risk assessment and reporting package does Council want?
6. Which **specific** categories/limits of insurance, certificates, additional-insured status and indemnity would Council require?
7. What independent sanitisation evidence/ASD/NIST alignment, audit rights, breach-response and retention periods would Council and its privacy/cyber teams require?
8. Would Council consider an **off-site direct-donor proof-of-concept** unrelated to its contracted waste flow as initial evidence, without implying endorsement?
9. Is an incumbent-authorised subcontract or separately tendered service the preferable route, and when can a lawful approach to market take place?
10. If no pathway is currently open, which documentation should be completed before resubmission and who is the designated contact?
