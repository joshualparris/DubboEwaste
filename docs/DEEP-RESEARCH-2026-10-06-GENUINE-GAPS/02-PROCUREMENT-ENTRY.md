# 02 — Procurement entry for a tiny regional ITAD operator

**Questions answered:** 5–8  
**Research date:** 6 October 2026

## 5. Which portals/panels matter?

### NSW Government — Supplier Hub / buy.nsw

Supplier Hub is the primary discovery/profile layer. Registration requires a business identity and normally an ABN, contact details, business type/size, legal disclosures and products/services.

A fuller profile can include:

- licences/accreditations;
- capabilities;
- references/case studies;
- government credentials;
- team details.

Sources:
- https://buy.nsw.gov.au/help/getting-started
- https://buy.nsw.gov.au/supplier-guidance/supply-to-government/smes

### NSW whole-of-government device disposal

Contract **9826 — ICT End User Devices and Services** is the important established channel for device disposal/sanitisation. It covers:

- recovery from a customer site/staff work location;
- confirmation of sanitisation;
- disposal;
- updating asset-register data;
- reuse through sale/donation;
- supply-chain recovery;
- recycling through a proven third party;
- environmental certification.

Source:
https://www.info.buy.nsw.gov.au/contracts/ict-end-user-devices-and-services

A micro-operator should not assume it can simply join an active mandatory whole-of-government contract mid-term.

### Dubbo Regional Council — VendorPanel

Council says procurement over **$225,000 including GST** is publicly advertised through VendorPanel.

VendorPanel Marketplace registration is free for suppliers; suppliers choose categories and create a buyer-visible profile.

Sources:
- https://www.dubbo.nsw.gov.au/Business-Investors/Networks-and-Opportunities/doing-business-with-council
- https://www.vendorpanel.com.au/Marketplace

### Local Government Procurement (LGP)

LGP panels can provide statewide council access, but **suppliers can only join when the relevant RFT is released**; there is no rolling addition to an existing panel.

LGP recommends self-registration on TenderLink and eProcure for notifications and generally goes to market around six months before contract expiry unless an extension is taken.

Sources:
- https://lgp.org.au/help-centre
- https://lgp.org.au/tenders
- https://lgp.org.au/solutions-for-suppliers/become-an-approved-supplier

## 6. What registration/evidence is required?

### Entry-level discovery profile

For Supplier Hub:

- valid ABN/business;
- legal entity details;
- contact/business address;
- company type/size;
- legal disclosures;
- product/service categories.

The optional-but-important profile can add certifications, references and case studies.

### Tender/panel level

Requirements become contract-specific. Expect requests for some combination of:

- insurances;
- WHS;
- environmental controls;
- privacy/security;
- cyber controls;
- service capacity;
- subcontractors;
- modern slavery/supply-chain declarations;
- pricing;
- references;
- case studies;
- financial viability;
- technical capability.

Do not buy certifications merely because mature ITAD competitors advertise them. Capture requirements from real tenders before spending.

## 7. Which thresholds/preferences favour regional SMEs?

Current NSW guidance is unusually favourable at modest contract value.

For **non-construction goods/services**, government may buy directly from:

- a **regional business up to $150,000**;
- an **SME up to $250,000**.

At those values the business does not have to be on a scheme/contract merely because of the SME/regional policy, although other mandatory procurement arrangements can still apply.

Source:
https://buy.nsw.gov.au/supplier-guidance/supply-to-government/smes

This is strategically important. The first government opportunity for Dubbo ITAD does **not** need to be “win the whole NSW ITAD contract”.

### Dubbo Council local purchasing

Council's current public procurement page establishes VendorPanel and tender threshold, while a local-purchasing policy was under review in late September/October 2026. Treat draft local-preference settings as **draft until adopted**.

Practical implication: keep evidence of genuine local establishment/employment/capability, but do not price tenders assuming a proposed preference has legal effect.

## 8. How can a tiny operator enter?

### Route A — low-value direct buy

Best near-term route for:

- one-off local collection;
- small pilot;
- onsite inventory/triage;
- local transport/aggregation;
- low-volume reuse service.

The buyer still needs value for money and applicable policy compliance.

### Route B — subcontract to an established ITAD provider

This is probably the most credible institutional entry.

Potential local work:

- regional collection;
- asset count/scan;
- pallet/container preparation;
- onsite triage;
- secure transport to metro hub;
- approved testing/refurbishment;
- local redeployment where contract permits.

The existing repo already identifies national/NSW ITAD providers. This research shows **why subcontracting is structurally credible**: LGP itself documents authorised agents/subcontractors in some panel arrangements, and major procurement frameworks routinely support subcontracted delivery subject to contract terms.

### Route C — Council VendorPanel quotes

Register now even before a tender exists. Use a precise profile:

> Regional secure IT asset collection, serialised inventory, reuse-first triage, approved sanitisation coordination, refurbishment and documented downstream recycling.

Do not claim certifications/secure-destruction capabilities not yet held.

### Route D — wait for relevant LGP RFT

Monitor LGP tender calendar and register on TenderLink/eProcure. This is a medium-term route, not Phase 0.

## Minimum procurement-readiness pack

Before actively chasing institutional work, prepare:

1. ABN/legal entity/trading name clarity;
2. one-page capability statement;
3. service scope / explicit exclusions;
4. chain-of-custody sample;
5. data/sanitisation policy;
6. battery/safety policy;
7. insurance certificate once bound;
8. downstream evidence;
9. privacy/cyber summary;
10. sample asset report/certificate;
11. references from completed controlled pilots;
12. regional coverage/pricing model.

## Strongest strategic conclusion

The sensible ladder is:

**Supplier Hub + VendorPanel registration → small direct/local pilot → subcontract under established operator → references → relevant panel/tender later.**

Trying to buy enterprise certifications before completing the first evidence-rich pilot reverses the order.
