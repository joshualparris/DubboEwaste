# Dubbo Regional Council — informal information / GIPA request

Start as an **informal access request**. If Council says formal GIPA is required, reuse this narrow record description in DRC&ME.

Council access page:
https://www.dubbo.nsw.gov.au/about-council/meetings-and-documents/access-to-information

## Requested records

**Period:** 1 July 2025 to present.

I seek existing records relating to the collection, transport, processing and downstream destination of electronic waste collected through Dubbo Regional Council waste facilities after the Matthews Metals arrangement recorded as ending 30 June 2025.

Please provide, where held:

1. current contract, purchase order, RFQ award, service agreement or other procurement record identifying the e-waste service provider;
2. contractor legal name and ABN;
3. commencement/expiry dates;
4. service scope for Whylandra, Wellington and village/rural facilities;
5. any record identifying the first downstream e-waste processing facility/company;
6. aggregate e-waste tonnage/weight by financial year for 2022–23, 2023–24, 2024–25 and 2025–26;
7. where recorded, breakdown between household/public e-waste and Council-owned/corporate ICT equipment;
8. any current certificate-of-recycling, weight-docket or downstream-report template supplied to Council;
9. any record describing whether usable computers are assessed for reuse before recycling; and
10. any current arrangement governing embedded-battery devices/laptops.

I am not requesting personal information, individual resident/customer records, device serial numbers or commercially sensitive unit pricing if those details would prevent release. Redacted records or aggregate data are suitable.

The current public GIPA contracts register dated 28 September 2026 does not identify an e-waste contract valued at $150,000+, so this request specifically seeks operational/procurement records that may fall below that publication threshold or sit within another arrangement.
