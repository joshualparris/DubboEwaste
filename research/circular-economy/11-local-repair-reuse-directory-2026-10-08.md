## What this directory shows

Dubbo does not have “no repair economy”. It has a **distributed repair economy without a shared triage layer**.

A useful circular-economy pilot does not need to duplicate all these services. It could operate as:

> one front door → classify item → repair locally / donate / refurbish IT / stewardship scheme / materials recycling.

The strongest unmet product is therefore **routing and coordination**, plus electronics-specific reuse/data handling.
