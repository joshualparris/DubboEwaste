# Ingram Micro BlueIQ

Checked: 2026-10-02 · Evidence: EVD-BENCH-024 · Role: Client / ITAD platform

## Publicly advertised features

Official case studies describe BlueIQ asset tracking with a single client reporting portal. Historical official material describes role access, scheduling, process rules, custom fields and hierarchical asset relationships; partner announcement connects it with remarketing operations.

## Limits and unresolved details

Proprietary service/partner platform. Older collateral is historical scope; current portal access, APIs, valuation and booking features require confirmation. Not a freely available generic SaaS product.

## AssetFlow implications

Use this product as a benchmark in these domains: INV,PORT,INT. Build operational records and release controls in AssetFlow; use documented adapters for external device processing. Do not label a browser workflow as equivalent to its erasure engine, certification or physical automation.
