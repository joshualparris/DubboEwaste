# Spotify listening list — e-waste, reuse, refurbishment, ITAD and digital inclusion

**Compiled:** 2 October 2026 (AEST)  
**Purpose:** a curated listening list specifically for the Dubbo eWaste project.

This is **not** a generic sustainability-podcast list. The episodes below were selected because they directly overlap with the project's core questions:

- reuse before recycling;
- testing and grading old electronics;
- secure data wiping;
- corporate fleet recovery;
- IT asset disposition (ITAD);
- refurbishment/resale economics;
- right to repair;
- reverse logistics;
- downstream material recycling;
- digital inclusion / social reuse;
- Australian e-waste conditions.

## Listen first — the 10 most relevant

### 1. The Energy Forum — “Beyond Recycling: The Global Systems Behind E-Waste”
**Relevance: 5/5 — best overall match**

Spotify show: https://open.spotify.com/show/2zw9b4rg8SipySzQ7z54ld

**Why this is highly relevant**
- explicitly argues that e-waste is not just a recycling problem;
- compares reuse/refurbishment with material recycling;
- follows electronics through collection, standards, trade and recovery;
- discusses why only a minority of discarded electronics reaches formal recovery systems;
- directly supports the Dubbo eWaste thesis that the highest-value intervention occurs **before** destructive recycling.

This is probably the best broad overview for understanding why “reuse first, recycle second” is more than a slogan.

---

### 2. The Eco Sheroes Podcast — “Is Your Phone's Data Safe When You Recycle It? The Right Way to Do It”
**Relevance: 5/5 — closest match to the actual proposed workflow**

Spotify show: https://open.spotify.com/show/0ZYeGIT5uL9WotjuovC3J1  
Episode date: 5 May 2026 — 48:57

**Why this is highly relevant**
The episode covers almost the exact Dubbo eWaste operating sequence:
- grading second-hand electronics;
- data wiping versus merely formatting/factory resetting;
- planned obsolescence;
- repair economics;
- how to buy/sell refurbished devices safely;
- environmental benefit of refurbished electronics;
- **repair vs reuse vs recycle**;
- reverse logistics.

The guest's operating principle is essentially:

> **repair first → reuse next → recycle last**

That is very close to the Phase 0 model in this repo.

**Relevance: 5/5 — best commercial ITAD/refurbishment episode**

Spotify: https://open.spotify.com/episode/27XCbC3CjrJ2Wy5nCoYjlq

### 4. Green IO — “Why we hate recycling” with Elaine Brown and Ross Cockburn
**Relevance: 5/5 — strongest reuse-before-recycling argument**

Spotify: https://open.spotify.com/episode/4TmYzRWMhOT3NQ1mamKi57

**Why this is highly relevant**
Elaine Brown runs the Edinburgh Remakery, which refurbished hundreds of laptops rather than simply recycling them.

The episode is directly about:
- wasted value in computers and phones;
- refurbishment;
- right to repair;
- why recycling can happen **too early**;
- extending useful device life.

This is especially relevant to the concern that functional laptops can lose hundreds of dollars of whole-device value once they enter an end-of-life stream.

# What these podcasts collectively reinforce

Across very different countries and businesses, the same model repeatedly appears:

**get the device before it becomes anonymous waste**
→ identify it
→ protect the data
→ test it
→ grade it
→ preserve whole-device value
→ repair/refurbish where economic
→ remarket/redeploy/donate
→ recover parts
→ materially recycle only the final remainder.

That is extremely close to the business architecture developed throughout this repository.
