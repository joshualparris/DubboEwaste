- **No Australian ITAD company has a substantive process video.** Fliptech's channel has short clips and conference interviews; Renew IT, WorkVentures and Reconnect have promo-length videos only. The *floor process* has to come from US facility tours (E2–E3) or a site visit (see 09).
- A Bendigo E-Waste YouTube presence wasn't found.
- Repair channels (NorthridgeFix, Rossmann) are mostly **board-level**, beyond Phase 0. Watch them to learn *what to refuse*, not to copy.
- **[Open]:** watch A1, A6, B1, D1 and E2 first (about 2.5 hours total), then update the triage matrix in 01 with anything that changes a rule.
