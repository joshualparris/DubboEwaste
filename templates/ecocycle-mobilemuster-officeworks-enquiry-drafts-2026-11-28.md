## 1. EcoCycle — certified downstream and battery route (send first)

**To:** EcoCycle partnerships (via ecocycle.com.au contact form; attention Spyro Kalos, National Partnerships Manager ANZ, if still in role)
**Subject:** Downstream recycling and battery route for a small Dubbo refurbishment pilot

Hi Spyro,

I'm setting up a small, reuse-first computer refurbishment pilot in Dubbo, NSW. We'll only take equipment that owners give us in writing before disposal — we're not asking for anything from your collection streams.

Anything we can't safely reuse needs a certified destination, and I'd like that to be a recycler with your accreditation. Could you help with a few questions?

1. Would EcoCycle or Eco E-Waste accept small, sorted batches of end-of-life computers, phones and batteries from a regional refurbisher, and on what terms?
2. What paperwork comes back — weight receipts, recycling or destruction certificates, serial-level records?
3. How do you want lithium batteries and embedded-battery devices packed and transported?
4. Which EcoCycle sites hold AS/NZS 5377 and R2v3, and does your R2v3 scope include any test-and-repair process?
5. Do any of your collection programmes already route working devices to reuse partners before recycling? If so, is there a way a regional partner could be part of that?

Happy to talk by phone if that's easier.

Kind regards,
