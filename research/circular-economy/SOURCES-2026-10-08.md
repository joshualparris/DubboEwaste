## Extraction note

The adopted Waste Strategy is a large PDF. During this research pass the full PDF could not be opened by the web PDF renderer due to its file size, so numeric tables were cross-checked against the search index's extracted public text. Where a decision depends on a precise table value, retrieve the original Council PDF locally and verify the relevant page directly.
