# Draft Council briefing — DubboEwaste reuse-first pilot

**Prepared following Josh's discussion with Anel, 13 October 2026.**  
**Status:** Proposed presentation/briefing structure, **not yet emailed** and not Council-approved.

## Purpose
Show Council how an accountable Dubbo-based pilot could receive unwanted IT devices, protect personal data, maximise useful reuse, donate suitable equipment and responsibly recycle the remainder. Seek feedback on an appropriate partnership, collections permissions, safety controls and destination organisations.

## Proposed process — from pickup to final destination
1. **Enquiry and acceptance:** Agree accepted device categories, donor eligibility, ownership/authority to transfer, volumes, condition and collection appointment. Do not accept suspected stolen devices, dangerous batteries or unknown hazardous items.
2. **Collection and transport:** Record donor, pickup time, custody handover, device count and safe transport measures. Identify battery damage and segregate unsafe units; avoid collecting dangerous items outside a suitable handling process.
3. **Intake and tracking:** Assign unique AssetFlow job/lot/asset IDs; photograph and record make/model/serial (privately), source, ownership, storage location and initial condition. No assertions that missing drives were sanitised.
4. **Quarantine / triage:** Identify damaged/swollen batteries, corrosion, mains hazards and unknown data-bearing media. Control access to storage. Separate unsafe items and follow appropriate recycling guidance.
5. **Data-bearing media decision:** Identify SSDs, HDDs, removable media and embedded storage; record data ownership/permission. **No drive supplied** is distinct from **drive verified erased**. Use sanitisation appropriate to media and planned disposition, with verification and evidence retained; cryptographic erase, overwrite or physical destruction as appropriate. If verification fails, do not redistribute the storage.
6. **Hardware evaluation:** Diagnose power, screen, RAM, storage, ports, thermal behaviour and general serviceability with competent procedures.
7. **Refurbishment and testing:** Clean, repair, install supported software if lawful and suitable, run functional/safety checks, record limitations and repair evidence. Avoid unsupported safety claims or unlicensed software.
8. **Reuse-first disposition:** Prioritise a **potential donation pathway** to eligible community groups/families with recipient eligibility, accessibility, reliable condition disclosures, suitable warranty/consumer law review and secure handover. Compare donation and sale honestly, including sustainability and funding impacts.
9. **Recycle last:** Send non-reusable equipment, batteries and data-bearing components to appropriately vetted recycling channels; retain downstream receipts/certificates where available and validate actual destination.
10. **Reporting:** AssetFlow audit trail links source → transfer → media sanitisation outcome → repair/testing → donation/sale/recycling → destination/recipient acknowledgement. Track device numbers, reuse rates, material diversion, volunteer training and incidents.

## Decisions requested from Council / Anel
- Can Council support a limited pilot or introduce an auspicing organisation?
- Where may collection, temporary storage and repair occur? Which consents, land-use, waste transport/storage rules and fire protections apply?
- Is equipment donation preferable to resale for target beneficiaries, and what recipient organisations might be appropriate?
- What format of verified media sanitisation evidence and transfer records does Council expect?
- Which grant streams or in-kind support could fund safe batteries, testing, PPE, tools, materials and insurance?
- Can children/NDIS participants join supervised skills-building activities, and what safeguards/partners are needed?
