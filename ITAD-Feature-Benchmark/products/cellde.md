# CellDe SmartSuite

Checked: 2026-10-02 · Evidence: EVD-BENCH-015 · Role: Mobile

## Publicly advertised features

Smart Diagnostics device identification, automated/interactive tests and custom profiles; Smart Check lost/stolen, blocklist, SIM/memory-card and lock checks; Smart Wipe; multi-device USB erasure; progress dashboard; report filtering and exports; Smart Transfer cross-platform content migration.

## Limits and unresolved details

Content transfer needs separate customer authority and privacy controls. Lock checking is not blanket authority or ability to remove activation locks.

## AssetFlow implications

Use this product as a benchmark in these domains: MOBILE,DATA,DIAG,INT. Build operational records and release controls in AssetFlow; use documented adapters for external device processing. Do not label a browser workflow as equivalent to its erasure engine, certification or physical automation.
