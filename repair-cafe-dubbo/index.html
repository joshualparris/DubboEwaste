A little less throwaway

It’s not a repair shop. It’s a community.

Repair Cafés bring people with broken things together with people who enjoy fixing them. You stay with your item, ask questions and learn as you go.

Sometimes it gets fixed. Sometimes you leave knowing more than you did before. Both matter.

What could you bring?

From torn jeans to tired tech.

Our first repair categories will depend on volunteer skills, venue approval and insurance. These are possibilities, not yet confirmed services.

🧵

Clothing & textiles

Buttons, seams, small tears and everyday mending.

💻

Computers & tech

Basic troubleshooting, software advice and safe simple fixes.

🧸

Household favourites

Small furniture, toys and non-powered objects.

🚲

Bikes & mechanics

Simple maintenance, subject to competence and safety.

Safety comes first. This won’t be an unattended drop-off service. Repairs aren’t guaranteed. We won’t offer mains electrical, lithium-battery or safety-critical repairs without specific approval, appropriately skilled volunteers and written insurance cover. Please don't bring an item until an event is announced.

Good to know

A few things people ask.

Is Repair Café Dubbo running yet?

Not yet. We're working towards a safe volunteer-led pilot. We'll publish confirmed event details here once the venue, insurance and volunteer arrangements are in place.

Will repairs be free?

We intend to offer free collaborative volunteer help, with optional donations once suitable governance and banking are in place. Visitors may need to supply replacement parts. Final terms will be confirmed before launch.

Can you take my item away?

No. The idea is to stay with your item and learn together. Volunteers can decline repairs for safety or competence reasons.

Can beginners volunteer?

Absolutely. Organisers, greeters and learners are just as welcome as experienced fixers.

Are you an incorporated group or registered charity?

Not currently. We're an emerging community group exploring suitable insurance and auspicing arrangements. Donations are not represented as tax deductible.

Are you officially affiliated with the international Repair Café organisation?

We are developing a local repair community and do not currently claim formal affiliation with the international organisation.
