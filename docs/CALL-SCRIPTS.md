# Call list, in order

## 1. NSW Fair Trading - 13 32 20
- "I plan to take donated, business-owned laptops, phones and tablets for free, wipe and refurbish them, resell the good ones, and recycle the rest. Is that a 'recycling and rubbish collection program' that doesn't need a second-hand dealer licence?"
- "If I do need a licence, can I wipe or repair devices during the 14-day hold?"
- "Does a donated item count as 'acquired'? Are game consoles covered?"
- "Can the licence be held by a business that already has one, with my shed added as a notified premises?"
- Ask for the answer in writing, or how to apply for an exemption (licensing.exemptions@customerservice.nsw.gov.au).

## 2. Dubbo Regional Council - planning
- "What's the zoning of my property? Is a home business with business-client pickups by appointment, plus online sales, permitted without consent?"
- "What triggers a DA if the public drops off or buys on set days?"
- "Is storing e-waste in a shed a waste facility use under the LEP?"

## 3. NSW EPA - 131 555
- Thresholds for refurbishing/dismantling small volumes; do damaged lithium batteries count as hazardous waste for storage?

## 4. Joe (Bendigo E-Waste)
- What exactly counts as "good", what he pays for sorted lots, minimum volumes, freight.

## 5. Matthews Metal Management (Dubbo)
- Small commercial e-waste loads, TVs/printers, price or free, hours.

## 6. Whylandra (Council waste)
- Commercial e-waste fee, NTCRS limits for a business drop-off.

## 7. Insurers
- Public liability and product liability for a home shed e-waste refurb with visitors; lithium battery conditions.

## 8. Avance (and any related entity)
- Conflict-of-interest check; does the business hold a dealer licence; would it agree in writing to anything involving its entity.
