# Document Retention Policy

**Interim conservative policy** pending legal/accounting advice.

## Keep permanently while business operates + archive
- asset provenance / ownership authority;
- serial/IMEI intake register;
- data sanitisation/destruction records;
- downstream certificates/dockets;
- sale/recall traceability;
- regulator approvals/exemptions.

## Financial/tax records
Retain for the period required by current ATO/business law for the entity; confirm with accountant. Do not shorten retention based on this draft.

## Customer/support records
Keep enough to support ACL/warranty/recall obligations and dispute history while minimising unnecessary personal data.

## Photos
Keep intake/condition/defect photos linked to asset ID while needed for provenance, listing, warranty or dispute evidence.

## Security
- restrict supplier IDs/contact data;
- do not store donor passwords;
- do not copy donor personal files;
- back up records;
- document destruction of records when authorised retention ends.

## Naming
`YYYYMMDD_assetID_record-type`
