# ZenAdmin

Checked: 2026-10-02 · Evidence: EVD-BENCH-014 · Role: Lifecycle

## Publicly advertised features

Procurement-to-retirement device operations; employee onboarding/offboarding; retrieval, logistics and storage; inventory and reassignment; endpoint/MDM, identity, SaaS and IT support services in one platform.

## Limits and unresolved details

Lifecycle service fulfilment is different from locally operated ITAD processing. Confirm country coverage and which capabilities are software versus managed services.

## AssetFlow implications

Use this product as a benchmark in these domains: LIFE,LOG,INV,INT. Build operational records and release controls in AssetFlow; use documented adapters for external device processing. Do not label a browser workflow as equivalent to its erasure engine, certification or physical automation.
