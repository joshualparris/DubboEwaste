## Case studies (most likely path today — INFERENCE unless noted)
1. **5-yr-old laptop, failed battery.** Repair (local technician; battery cost vs laptop value) → if declined: Marketplace sale as "parts/repair" or drawer → else Whylandra CRC e-waste → scheme → M. *Improve:* Repair Café battery clinic; refurb social enterprise; Council e-waste reuse bin with data wipe.
2. **10-yr-old washer not draining.** Often a pump/hose blockage (cheap, DIY-able) → but owner often replaces; old washer → scrap merchant or Whylandra (degassing n/a) or kerbside clean-up → M. *Improve:* appliance triage volunteers, parts donor from scrap yards.
3. **Working couch.** Marketplace/hand-down → charity (shop-dependent; Vinnies may refuse; many shops lack furniture sales) → kerbside/Whylandra → L (bulky). *Improve:* reuse area; Men's Shed or charity partnership; pickup.
4. **Bike needing tyres/brakes.** Bike shop or DIY; if abandoned, Council guidance sends it to depot → scrap [VERIFIED guidance]. *Improve:* bike-refurb pathway (Council + Men's Shed/youth service/Repair Café).
5. **Broken cordless drill.** Replace (battery ecosystem) → battery to CRC [VERIFIED] → drill to scrap/e-waste M. *Improve:* battery swap/repair clinic; tool library.
6. **Office replaces 25 computers.** Options: lease return, ITAD vendor, employee buy-back, donate, recycler. Local practice [UNVERIFIED]. Data wipe certification is the key barrier. *Improve:* local certified wipe-and-refurb service (fits DubboEwaste).
7. **Renovation (doors/timber/cabinets).** Skip bin → Whylandra C&D fees [VERIFIED listing] → recycling/landfill; salvage only if owner lists items free online. *Improve:* deconstruction guidance + building-reuse yard.
8. **Farm replaces machinery.** Trade-in to dealer → used market/auction → parts/rebuild → scrap. [INFERENCE] Mature loop; study it.
