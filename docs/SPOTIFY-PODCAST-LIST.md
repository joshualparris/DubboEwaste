# Spotify podcast listening guide — Dubbo E-Waste, reuse, ITAD, repair, resale and creative microbusiness

**Curated and link-checked:** 2 October 2026  
**Purpose:** a practical Spotify listening list covering the major themes developed across the Dubbo E-Waste repo and related chats.

This is not a generic sustainability playlist. Each item is included because it maps to a specific project question:

- reuse before recycling;
- IT asset disposition (ITAD);
- secure data handling;
- chain of custody;
- refurbishment economics;
- right to repair;
- e-waste processing;
- digital inclusion/social reuse;
- resale/flipping;
- proving demand before holding stock;
- starting a tiny home-based business;
- Kristy's art/creative-business pathway.

Where a direct Spotify episode link was available, it is used. Where Spotify surfaced only a show page, the exact relevant episode is named.

---

# Start here — the 10 most relevant

## 1. All Things Circular — EP 59: Refurbished Technology, ITAD, Circular Growth & AI Innovation with Martin Series of Foxway

Spotify:  
https://open.spotify.com/episode/27XCbC3CjrJ2Wy5nCoYjlq

**Why this is probably the single most relevant episode:** it directly covers the model Dubbo E-Waste has evolved toward:

- ITAD;
- device lifecycle management;
- refurbishment;
- circular technology services;
- extracting value before end-of-life recycling.

**Repo connection:** this sits almost exactly between the Joe/Sircel/WorkVentures/PonyUp models and the proposed Dubbo service.

**Listen for:** how a mature circular-tech operator decides between reuse, refurbishment and disposal, and where margin/value actually comes from.

3. **Operational ITAM — Hardware Asset Management: The Seven Gates**

# What each cluster can teach Dubbo E-Waste

| Cluster | Main question it helps answer |
|---|---|
| ITAD / asset recovery | How do businesses retire equipment professionally rather than throw it out? |
| E-waste / circular economy | What happens after reuse fails, and where is the material value? |
| Repair / right to repair | Which devices deserve repair instead of replacement? |
| Social reuse | How can recovered devices create community value? |
| Flipping / ecommerce | Does the unit economics actually work? |
| Creative business | How can a hobby grow slowly without premature overhead? |
| Data / sanitisation | What does responsible custody of client data look like? |

# Important limitations

- Most ITAD/e-waste episodes are international. Use them for **operating models and questions**, not NSW legal requirements.
- Podcast claims are not treated as regulatory evidence in this repository.
- The repo's legal, planning, data and safety decisions should continue to rely on the primary-source research files.
- Spotify availability can change.
- Some Spotify search results expose only a **show** page rather than a direct episode URL; the exact relevant episode title is included above.
