# DRAFT: Dubbo eWaste reuse and recycling program charter

**Status: DRAFT, 28 November 2026.** This charter describes how a genuine program would run. **It does not create an exemption.** Clause 33 of the Pawnbrokers and Second-hand Dealers Regulation 2026 sets no criteria for a "recycling program". Whether it covers this model is unconfirmed until Fair Trading answers in writing ([research](../docs/RECYCLING-PROGRAM-EXEMPTION-RESEARCH-2026-11-28.md)). Adopt it only if it's true of what you actually do. A charter written to look like a program, over what is really trading, would be worse than no charter.

Each item is marked:
- **[LAW]**: required by some law (named);
- **[POLICY]**: our own rule, needed for the program to be what it says it is;
- **[BEST PRACTICE]**: voluntary.

## 1. Purpose [POLICY]
Keep usable electronics in use and out of landfill in the Dubbo region. Reuse comes first, then responsible recycling of whatever can't be reused.

## 2. What we collect [POLICY]
Computers, laptops, tablets, phones and accessories, offered **free of charge** by households, businesses and organisations. We don't pay for items in the program stream. We don't accept e-bikes or e-scooters, or swollen or damaged lithium batteries (see the [intake policy](../docs/INTAKE-POLICY.md)).

## 3. How people give items [POLICY]
A published way to offer items (website or page), with appointment-only receiving at [premises]. No general public drop-off until premises approval allows it.

## 4. Ownership transfer and donor consent [POLICY] [BEST PRACTICE]
The donor signs a transfer (see [asset-transfer-and-chain-of-custody.md](asset-transfer-and-chain-of-custody.md)). It confirms they own the item or are authorised to give it, that it's given free, and that data will be erased. No signature, no intake.

## 5. Records [POLICY]
Every item gets an AssetFlow record: intake date, donor reference, make, model, serial or IMEI, condition, outcome (reused, sold, donated, parts, recycled) and destination. Records are kept for at least 3 years. We check serials and IMEIs against available lost-or-stolen checks [BEST PRACTICE, aligned with the Act's purpose]. If an item is suspected stolen, we hold it and contact police.

## 6. Data sanitisation [POLICY] [BEST PRACTICE]
Erase every storage device with a method suited to the media (NIST SP 800-88 Rev 2 as the reference), verify it, and record the result. No copies of donor data are ever made or kept. Media that can't be erased are destroyed and recorded.

## 7. Refurbishment and reuse [POLICY]
Test, repair and reset. Devices still locked to an account (Activation Lock, FRP, MDM) are not passed on until the owner releases them through official channels.

## 8. Where reusable devices go [POLICY]
In order of preference:
1. Gifted to people and community organisations in need.
2. Sold at modest prices to cover program costs, **only** if Fair Trading confirms clause 33 covers this, or under a licence.

Prices and the use of proceeds are published in an annual summary.

## 9. Parts harvesting [POLICY]
Parts from collected devices are used in repairs or recorded as outcomes. Selling parts separately waits on Fair Trading's answer to the parts question.

## 10. Responsible recycling [LAW: POEO Act s 143, lawful destination for waste] [POLICY]
Items that can't be reused go to recyclers lawfully able to accept them (AS/NZS 5377 certified where possible). We keep dockets. Batteries go to an accepting battery collection point.

## 11. No purchased stock in the program [POLICY]
Items bought from anyone are not program goods. If any trading in bought goods happens, it must be licensed and kept completely separate: separate records, separate stock and no mixing.

## 12. Selling safely [LAW: Australian Consumer Law; Gas and Electricity (Consumer Safety) Act 2017]
Consumer guarantees apply to goods sold in trade. Second-hand electrical items must have been approved when first sold new. No sale of e-micromobility devices that don't meet the 2026 NSW requirements.

## 13. Reporting [BEST PRACTICE]
A yearly summary: items received, reused, gifted, sold and recycled; tonnes recycled; any incidents.

## 14. Money [POLICY] [LAW: tax]
Keep separate accounts and records of income and costs. Proceeds go to running costs, as stated in the yearly summary.

## 15. Review [POLICY]
Review this charter on Fair Trading's written reply, and yearly after that.
