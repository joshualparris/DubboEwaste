# Total Recall Envision / DHS Worldwide

Checked: 2026-10-02 · Evidence: EVD-BENCH-005 · Role: ERP

## Publicly advertised features

Unified ITAD scheduling, billing, workflows and inventory; barcode and location tracking of individual items, pallets and bulk containers; condition/status reporting. Separate shredding suite advertises recurring services, route planning, driver tracking, signatures, CRM and client invoice access.

## Limits and unresolved details

Adjacent shredding and records modules are not automatically included in an ITAD licence. Confirm packaging, ERP exports and erasure interfaces.

## AssetFlow implications

Use this product as a benchmark in these domains: LOG,INV,FIN,RECYCLE,PORT. Build operational records and release controls in AssetFlow; use documented adapters for external device processing. Do not label a browser workflow as equivalent to its erasure engine, certification or physical automation.
