## Most consequential findings

### Planning

Current evidence strongly points to the proposed residential site being in an **R2 Low Density Residential** context, but the authoritative property-specific confirmation should still come from NSW Spatial Viewer or a Dubbo Regional Council s10.7 certificate.

If R2 is confirmed:
- home business and home industry appear in the R2 consent-use table
- unlisted principal uses are prohibited
- goods repair and reuse premises / resource recovery / waste transfer uses do not appear as R2 principal uses.

A qualifying home business may nevertheless be exempt development if all Codes SEPP standards are satisfied.

**Practical consequence:** keep Phase 0 genuinely low-intensity and obtain written Council classification before public opening.

### Waste law

An unwanted item can still legally be “waste” even where it will be reused, refurbished or recycled.

EPA licensing thresholds are enormous compared with a 20–30-device pilot, but being below an EPL threshold does **not** remove planning-law requirements.

### Second-hand dealer law

The current Fair Trading recycling/rubbish-program carve-out is real, but public sources do not resolve whether:

**collect free → triage → refurbish/resell good devices → recycle remainder**

falls inside it.

Do not pay the licence fee or design around the 14-day holding rule until Fair Trading answers the exact model.

### Scrap metal

A computer is not automatically “scrap metal” merely because it contains metal. NSW's statutory definition also requires that it be sold/obtained **as scrap for recycling/reprocessing of the metal**.

That supports keeping Phase 0 explicitly reuse/refurbishment-first.

### AMR / Matthews

The former Matthews Dubbo yard changed from OneSteel/InfraBuild/Matthews registration to Scrap Metal Services / Australian Metal Recycling between **17 July 2025 and 19 February 2026**.

AMR currently presents the Dubbo site as **“Formerly Matthews Metal Management.”**

The exact corporate transaction and exact e-waste downstream processor remain unpublished.

### Council contractor

Dubbo Regional Council's last publicly documented Matthews e-waste collection contract expired **30 June 2025**.

A current successor contract/novation could not be identified online. This is now a targeted Council/GIPA question rather than a general research gap.

### NTCRS

The NTCRS is potentially the best zero/low-cost residual stream for:
- TVs
- PCs/laptops
- tablets
- monitors
- printers
- drives
- many computer peripherals/parts.

TechCollect explicitly offers selected collection partners free collection infrastructure, logistics and recycling.

### Batteries

Do not launch as a public battery collection point.

B-cycle participation requires a formal accredited-collector relationship, risk assessment/safety plan and container process, and damaged/swollen batteries cannot simply go into an ordinary B-cycle drop point.

### NetWaste

NetWaste currently says **Sims Metal Management collects e-waste from the majority of NetWaste councils, while several councils have individual arrangements**.

Do not assign Sims to every member council.

The old 2021–23 Sims/McCabe/St Marys contract material is historical evidence, not proof of the exact current 2026 chain.

### Actual Dubbo volumes

Verified Council annual-report figures:

- **2021/22: 27 tonnes e-waste**
- **2022/23: 18 tonnes e-waste**

These are Council-managed tonnes sent off-site, **not total Dubbo e-waste generation**.

Later Council reporting does not provide the same itemised e-waste breakout.

### Supply sources

#### NSW public schools
Not a casual donation source. Current Department policy says schools use a **dedicated Department eWaste contract** through EDConnect after secure wiping.

#### NSW Government
The NSW Device Bank launched in 2026 and is already redirecting government laptops into secure refurbishment/reuse. Its pilot tender disclosed about:
- 2,800 devices initially in storage
- ~2,500 more expected over 12 months.

Government surplus is becoming more structured, not less.

#### Charles Sturt University / NSW Health
Both have controlled disposal/data processes. Treat them as future formal-client/procurement opportunities, not informal device sources.

#### Best Phase 0 source
After the compliance gates are resolved, the best early supply targets are:
- households with pre-screened quality devices
- SMEs
- farms/agri businesses
- professional offices
- clubs/charities/community organisations
- independent/private organisations with authorised asset-disposal processes.
