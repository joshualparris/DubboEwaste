# Phase 0 written approval request pack

Use these to close the three external gates. Keep the exact home address/private details out of the public repo and include them only in the direct enquiry where required.

## A. Dubbo Regional Council planning

**Subject:** Written planning advice — small home-based computer refurbishment activity

Describe:
- appointment-only laptop/desktop refurbishment;
- expected frequency and maximum devices stored;
- no general household e-waste;
- no unattended public drop-off;
- limited customer visits/pickups;
- lithium screening/refusal controls;
- online resale;
- hours;
- storage/work area;
- no industrial shredding/demanufacturing.

Ask Council to confirm:
1. likely land-use classification;
2. whether exempt development/home-business provisions can apply;
3. whether DA/consent is required;
4. maximum floor area/storage/traffic/signage/noise conditions;
5. whether temporary holding of rejected devices before lawful downstream transfer changes classification.

## B. NSW Fair Trading

**Subject:** Second-hand dealer licensing question — reuse-first computer refurbishment model

Describe exactly how title is acquired:
- free transfer / purchase / business disposal;
- serialised intake;
- data sanitisation;
- refurbishment;
- resale/donation/parts/recycling.

Ask:
1. whether the model requires a second-hand dealer licence;
2. whether any recycling-program exclusion applies and under what facts;
3. whether a holding period applies to laptops/desktops;
4. whether sanitisation, SSD replacement, RAM upgrade or repair counts as prohibited alteration during any hold;
5. what ID/record/police-reporting rules apply;
6. whether business-to-business acquisitions differ.

Request a written response/reference.

## C. Insurer / broker

Disclose all activities:
- home-based electronics refurbishment;
- third-party/business-owned stock;
- data-bearing devices;
- lithium-ion batteries;
- appointment visitors/collections;
- resale of refurbished computers;
- parts replacement;
- data sanitisation;
- transport/collection;
- Repair Café excluded unless separately agreed.

Ask for written confirmation of:
1. public liability;
2. product liability;
3. customer goods / property in care, custody and control;
4. stock/tools at home;
5. fire/lithium exclusions or storage limits;
6. cyber/privacy/data-handling exclusions;
7. collection/transport cover;
8. second-hand/refurbished sales cover;
9. volunteer/community-event exclusion;
10. any required electrical testing, qualifications or risk controls.

Record final written answers in **AssetFlow /validation → APPROVALS**, and private quote details under **Commercial terms**.
