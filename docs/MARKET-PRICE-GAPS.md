# Remaining Market-Price Gaps

## Used TVs
Public Australian listings show very wide asking-price dispersion, so TVs must be priced by exact model/age/panel type rather than screen size alone.

Example current 55-inch Sydney asking prices observed in late Sep/Oct 2026:
- older/basic working 55-inch sets: often around **$100–$300**;
- midrange newer 4K sets: roughly **$300–$500**;
- OLED/QNED/premium sets: often **$600–$850+**;
- faulty/panel-damaged units can be near free/parts value.

Source examples:
- https://www.gumtree.com.au/s-tvs/sydney/55%2Btv/k0c21115l3003435
- https://www.gumtree.com.au/s-tvs/samsung%2B55/k0c21115

These are **asking prices, not sold prices**.

## Phones/tablets/laptops depreciation
Do not use a universal “3–4% per month” rule. Capture actual AU sold data in `data/ebay-sold-price-template.csv` by model, storage/spec and condition.

## Floor prices
Compare every device against:
- Officeworks/Moorup trade-in where eligible;
- Mobile Monster/Mobile Guru quote;
- parts proceeds;
- The Board Guy published scrap floor.

## Data still blocked/account-specific
- eBay Product Research sold prices;
- exact local Facebook sold outcomes;
- trade-in quotes that require model/condition input.

## Operating rule
No acquisition price based only on current asking listings. Use sold evidence where available or apply a larger risk margin.
