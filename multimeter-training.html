Learn to troubleshoot with a multimeter

Seven video lessons for safe low-voltage testing of laptop chargers, laptop power circuits and desktop power supplies. Start with the basics, then move to practical diagnosis.

Workshop equipment: SCA Hobbyist Digital Multimeter — collected 8 October 2026.

The new charger tutorial is from a UK English-language creator; check playback on this page before treating it as verified. If YouTube blocks an individual player, use that lesson's direct YouTube link. See the evidence statement on each lesson. I cannot directly listen to every video or retrieve all audio tracks, so those individual limits are disclosed rather than falsely claimed as verified.
Safety first: Begin with low-voltage DC and disconnected-cable continuity checks only. Never open laptop chargers or desktop power supplies. Never measure continuity on a live circuit. Red probe goes to V/Ω, black to COM, when measuring voltage. Verify connector pinouts before testing.
