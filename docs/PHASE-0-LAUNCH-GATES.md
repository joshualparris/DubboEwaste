# Phase 0 launch gates

This is the canonical action sequence for a small DubboEwaste experiment. It converts the research into four buckets: what blocks intake, what blocks resale, what can be learned during a bounded pilot, and what belongs later.

**Default status:** do not advertise general public intake until the external gates are answered in writing.

## 1. BLOCKS INTAKE

These are stop conditions, not suggestions.

- [ ] Council has classified the exact proposed premises/use, storage and customer-arrival model in writing.
- [ ] The lease/landlord position permits the proposed activity and storage, or the activity is moved to suitable premises.
- [ ] The workspace is dry, secure, ventilated and suitable for valuable stock and unwiped data-bearing devices.
- [ ] Insurance confirms stock in custody, testing/repair, visitors, batteries, transit, resale/product liability, privacy incidents and relevant exclusions.
- [ ] Fair Trading has answered whether the model is exempt or requires a second-hand dealer licence, and how the 14-day hold interacts with wiping/repair.
- [ ] A conservative lithium-battery procedure and emergency escalation path exists; loose, damaged, swollen, wet or fire-affected lithium is refused.
- [ ] Every accepted category has a documented safe route if it is refused, fails, becomes locked or has no resale value.
- [ ] Intake paperwork records authority/ownership, asset ID, serial/IMEI where present, data state, battery/safety state and acceptance decision.
- [ ] Physical capacity is capped; full shelf/quarantine/downstream capacity means intake pauses.

**Immediate refusal conditions:** uncertain authority, damaged or unsafe battery, exposed electrical hazard, active liquid/mould contamination, unresolved account/MDM/Activation Lock where resale is intended, or no known route for the item.

## 2. BLOCKS RESALE

An item may be accepted for assessment but cannot be listed until every relevant line is complete.

- [ ] Ownership/authority record is complete and retained.
- [ ] Data-bearing media is sanitised using a documented, media-appropriate method, or the device is routed to destruction/parts.
- [ ] Apple Activation Lock, Android FRP, Windows Autopilot/MDM and Chromebook enrolment have been cleared legitimately or the item is not sold as a usable device.
- [ ] Diagnostics and actual faults are recorded on the bench sheet.
- [ ] Battery condition and any limitation are disclosed.
- [ ] Cosmetic/functional grade and included accessories are recorded.
- [ ] Windows/macOS/iOS/Android/ChromeOS support status is checked for the specific model.
- [ ] Price covers direct costs, labour target, platform/shipping costs and a warranty/return reserve.
- [ ] Listing passes the ACL-safe warranty, defect-disclosure and recall/traceability checklist.
- [ ] Buyer-facing serial/sale record is retained.

Never use “certified data destruction”, “military grade” or “AS 5377 certified” as marketing language unless the exact provider/process supports it.

## 3. CAN LEARN DURING PILOT

These questions are deliberately empirical and should not block a tightly controlled known-source experiment once the intake gates are satisfied.

- reusable/repairable rate by category and source;
- false accepts and false rejects at the front door;
- time to identify, wipe, test, repair, clean, list and sell;
- parts yield and actual parts value;
- battery/lock/OS failure rates;
- sale price, days-to-sale, returns and warranty events;
- gross margin per labour hour after all direct costs;
- percentage and weight routed to verified recycling;
- downstream fees, rejected loads and evidence returned;
- supplier repeat interest and community-partner demand.

**Pilot scope:** 20–30 known-authority items; no public dumping, no damaged lithium, no unknown mixed commercial loads, no untracked private data. Review after items 5, 10, 20 and 30. Stop immediately for uncontrolled safety/data incidents, storage-capacity breach, repeated ownership ambiguity or loss of a safe route.

## 4. LATER / SCALE-UP

Do not buy or promise these before the pilot supports them:

- public unattended drop-off;
- broad household intake;
- loose-battery collection;
- CRT or hazardous-material expansion without confirmed route;
- industrial dismantling/shredding;
- warehouse or vehicle lease;
- staff or volunteers handling customer data;
- school/council/institutional volume;
- local sanitisation under another company's certification;
- Device Bank/Good360/community distribution commitments;
- a full Dubbo ITAD branch or downstream processing plant.

Scale only when the current gate remains closed-safe, stock turns within capacity, labour earns the target return, and every residual stream has current written terms.
