# ITADCollect

Checked: 2026-10-02 · Evidence: EVD-BENCH-006 · Role: ERP

## Publicly advertised features

Client collection amendments and live progress; downloadable certificates; CRM follow-up tasks; CSV collections; collection documents and signatures; DYMO/Zebra labels; asset and individual-drive tracking; erasure imports; specifications/photos/values/carbon fields; disposition; filtered resale stock; individual/bulk sales and invoices.

## Limits and unresolved details

Integration logos confirm advertised connections, not schemas or end-to-end reliability. Carbon fields alone do not establish a validated methodology.

## AssetFlow implications

Use this product as a benchmark in these domains: CRM,LOG,INV,DATA,SALE,PORT,ESG. Build operational records and release controls in AssetFlow; use documented adapters for external device processing. Do not label a browser workflow as equivalent to its erasure engine, certification or physical automation.
