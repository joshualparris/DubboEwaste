# Deeper Research Delta — 2 Oct 2026

This file records additional online research after the 68-question pass.

## AMR / Council / Education / Device Bank / Sircel

### AMR downstream
No new public source was found that names the exact first downstream e-waste processor receiving material from AMR's Dubbo yard. The correct position remains: AMR Dubbo accepts e-waste, but its downstream chain and current NTCRS/AS 5377 role are not publicly established.

### Dubbo Council contract
Council's current public procurement page routes tenders through VendorPanel. No publicly indexed post-30-Jun-2025 e-waste award was found. Written Council confirmation remains the cleanest evidence.

Source: https://www.dubbo.nsw.gov.au/Business-Investors/Networks-and-Opportunities/doing-business-with-council

### NSW Education
NSW Department of Education's current Technology in schools procedures confirm that the Department has a **dedicated eWaste contract** for secure disposal of digital assets and schools request collection through EDConnect. The public procedure does **not name the vendor**.

Source: https://education.nsw.gov.au/policy-library/policies/pd-2024-0481-01

### NSW Device Bank
The Device Bank remains a NSW Telco Authority + WorkVentures program. WorkVentures now has a current EOI page for NSW organisations/communities. This is the best public path for a Dubbo/Orana organisation to express interest; a named Dubbo partner still was not found.

Sources:
- https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/nsw-device-bank-to-help-people-and-communities-get-online
- https://workventures.com.au/nswdb-eoi/

### Good360
Good360's current Digital Divide program says devices are distributed through a vetted national network of charities/schools and refurbishment partners. No Dubbo-specific distribution record was found in the public material checked.

Sources:
- https://good360.org.au/digital-divide/
- https://good360.org.au/faq/

### Sircel
Sircel's own update confirms Sircel Limited and subsidiaries entered receivership and voluntary administration on 31 Oct 2025 and that operations continued under receivers/managers. No later reliable public source found in this pass establishes a completed sale or exit from external administration.

Source: https://sircel.com/2025/11/17/update-sircel-operations/
