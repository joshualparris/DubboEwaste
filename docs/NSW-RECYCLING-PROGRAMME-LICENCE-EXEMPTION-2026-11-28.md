# NSW second-hand dealer licensing: recycling programme exemption and low-cost pathways
28 November 2026 | Evidence-led public-source investigation | Not legal advice or permission to trade.

## Decision
Do not assume all electronics are exempt; do not spend $692 merely to resolve uncertainty. Seek written classification of the actual operation first, and maintain existing P0-I05 HOLD for activities that may require licensing. The strongest new evidence is NSW's own 2020 Regulatory Impact Statement (RIS), section 5.3 p13 and Appendix 4 p29: the proposed reform intentionally broadened the council-only exemption to all recycling and rubbish collection programmes, including community schemes. This was enacted in the 2021 Regulation cl6. September 2026 NSW guidance confirms recycling programmes remain exempt in principle. The exact current 2026 instrument's full wording was inaccessible; do not claim current cl6 text, eligibility, or formal approval. If classification fails, request a section 4A individual exemption and investigate the current equivalent of historical cl39 fee relief. No applications, communications, new sales, approvals or payments made.

## 2. What qualifies as a programme?
2021 cl6 and 2020 RIS support broader than council programmes, but no express definition, minimum volumes, registration, nonprofit or fee restriction identified in the retrieved historical cl6. Absence of an express condition does not settle how a court or regulator interprets programme, collection, sale or altered goods. Not verified under current 2026 text. A genuine organised reuse-first collection programme with documented owner consent, outcomes and lawful downstream routing is a plausible fact pattern, not an exemption certificate. Clarify sole trader, commercial operator, paid labour, retained profit, home location, appointment-based collection, paid acquisitions, parts, repairs, online sales, ITAD contracts, rejected items, reporting, scheme affiliation and whether prior approval is needed. No need to invent mandatory charity status, minimum volume, environmental accreditation or paperwork. Do not relabel bought-for-resale stock, commercial trading or existing private inventory retrospectively. Actual activities and dates matter.

## 5. Cost and decision paths (AUD, licence component only)
1. Pure customer-owned repair, no trading: $0 second-hand dealer fee if outside Act; other costs/obligations remain.
2. Bona fide recycling programme confirmed under current exclusion: $0 dealer licence fee; maintain title and evidence, legal/safety/consumer obligations. Not yet established.
3. Section 4A written whole/partial exemption: fee and timing not published in retrieved sources; ask whether prospective unlicensed applicant may apply against s7, scope, conditions, decision period/review rights. Partial relief may still require $692 licence.
4. Fee hardship relief: historical cl39; request current provision, evidence, whether processing component covered, deferral/instalments and decision before payment. No entitlement.
5. Formal service relationship with an existing licensed operator/community host: quote and role-dependent; written licence scope, ownership, agency, premises and reporting confirmation. Do not borrow licence or imply host automatically covers separate sales.
6. Ordinary licence: $692 initial, $494 online annual renewal; 3y $1574 (~$524.67/year), 5y $2456 (~$491.20/year), no assumption future fees fixed. At $20/$50/$100 incremental contribution per sale, first-year licence alone needs 35/14/7 sales respectively; excludes labour, overhead, parts, insurance, tax and returns. No actual profit data used. No confirmed low-turnover fee threshold, universal grant or concession. Service NSW's new Small Business Advisory program offers eligible businesses up to four hours free advice per financial year [24]; not legal determination. Ask about suitable legal referrals.

## 6. Draft programme charter (not adopted or approved)
Purpose: divert genuinely unwanted electronics from material recycling to safe reuse; repair where viable, donate or sell with accurate condition disclosures, route rejects to approved recyclers. Scope initially capped appointment-only owner-consented household/business donations of manageable computers/peripherals at an approved premises, subject to existing planning/insurance/ownership/hold gates. No public bins, deposited stock, unsafe batteries, unresolved title or accounts, mixed purchased resale stock, or government/customer assets without authority. Maintain distinct register for collected donations vs purchases/personal/customer repair; serial/source/consent/date, ownership transfer, custody, hold status, sanitisation validation, QA, outcome, costs/proceeds, recipient/receipt and downstream weight/receipt. Minimise private data; role controls, retention and secure disposal. No data browsing or sanitising/altering during a legally applicable hold absent written authority. Reuse before material recovery where safe and agreed; no claims of formal stewardship or accredited recycling without approval. Proceeds may be proposed to support documented operating costs and sustainable labour, with transparent accounting and no assumed profit restriction/exemption. Publish aggregate outcomes; no personal or donor information. Review before scaling; do not operate until exact activity gates cleared. This charter is a recommended evidence framework, not a statutory prerequisite or retroactive reclassification.
