## Reproducible economics (illustrative, not measured)

From the prior EcoCycle model: N incoming, p successful, $150 realised sale, $15 intake, $70 successful-device incremental cost, $5 reject cost. Contribution = N*(85p−20) − fixed overhead. For 30 devices at 35% expected yield (fractional expectation, not observed units): $292.50 before fixed costs, $-207.50 if $500 fixed overhead allocated. At N=30 and $500 fixed overhead, break-even p=(20+500/30)/85=43.14%; if acquisition averages $10 each, break-even 54.90%. This demonstrates why a larger-volume forecast is premature. Record actual prices, labour, freight, fees, repairs, returns, GST and reject receipts. No avoided CO2, tonnes or actual supplier figures inferred. Prior 30-device experiment criteria remain authoritative.
