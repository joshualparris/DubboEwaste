# Australian Electronics Shipping Matrix

**Updated:** 2 Oct 2026. Carrier rules change. Recheck before booking.

| Carrier | Used electronics with installed Li-ion | Loose/spare Li-ion | Air/Express | Key rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Australia Post | Conditional | Service-specific/conditional | Air restrictions apply | Use current AusPost dangerous-goods guidance for exact configuration/service |
| Sendle | Caution: public page clearly accepts small quantities of **brand-new** electronics with built-in batteries; used/refurb status needs written confirmation | Conditional domestically under stated road conditions | International Li-ion not accepted | Do not assume used refurb devices qualify |
| CouriersPlease | Conditional for pre-approved DG/EDI customers | Conditional DG process | Lithium not by air in its network | Road-only DG process, account approval |
| Aramex Australia | Standard network terms prohibit Dangerous Goods | Do not assume accepted | Do not assume | Obtain written confirmation before using for Li-ion electronics |
| StarTrack | Service/account-specific public detail incomplete | Unknown | Unknown | Get DG terms for exact StarTrack account/service |
| Pack & Send | Franchise/service-specific | Service-specific | Service-specific | Quote exact item/battery and obtain written acceptance |

## Booking checklist
1. Is battery installed, packed-with, or loose?
2. Is battery damaged/recalled/swollen? If yes: **do not ship through ordinary parcel service**.
3. Watt-hour rating / battery count known?
4. Used/refurbished or new?
5. Exact service travels by air or surface?
6. Required labels/declaration/account approval?
7. Carrier written acceptance retained for ambiguous cases.

## Policy
No employee/operator should infer one carrier's lithium rules from another carrier, or assume all Australia Post Group services share identical terms.
