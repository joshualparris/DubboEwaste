# Electronics repair & ITAD: 15-skill field-school pathway
Updated 11 October 2026. Applies to DubboEwaste, Repair Café Dubbo and shared learning.

This is an **educational pathway, not a licence, competency certificate, electrical safety clearance or authority to touch someone else's data**. Each course has three lessons, a conventional knowledge check, two additional adaptive scenario levels, and a practical task that may be submitted for directly observed supervisor review. The adaptive quiz is for learning, not authorisation.

## The right sequence

**Stage 1: safe foundations (courses 1–6).** Identify electrical and lithium hazards; build simple low-voltage circuits; learn meter jack and mode discipline; troubleshoot from evidence; disassemble without damaging connectors or ESD-sensitive parts.

**Stage 2: practical repair (courses 7–12).** Understand USB-C PD and DC input; follow schematics; build through-hole soldering quality; desolder scrap components; practise SMD on expendable PCBs; inspect and refurbish laptops, including iPad-style delicate ribbon and adhesive concepts.

**Stage 3: regulated/release decisions (courses 13–15).** Learn theory of appliance testing without self-certification; make auditable sanitisation/custody records; decide repair, salvage or recycle using genuine cost and safety evidence.

## High-quality trainers: open a video, then reflect on the technique and its limits

- **Afrotechmods:** [Basic electricity: resistance and Ohm's law](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NfcgA1axPLo), [How to use a multimeter](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bF3OyQ3HwfU). Patient beginner explanations and practical low-voltage circuits. Older multimeter models and equipment choices may not reflect current safety standards.
- **Mr SolderFix:** [Beginners guide to soldering](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dETS6YHrNRI), [Controlling solder joints](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sj5fZgsL8J0), [SMD crystal removal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QsWQrP69ZuI), [Wiring and fine soldering](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Npl10nkywsM). Excellent close-up demonstrations; do not copy fine-pitch or hot-air jobs on valuable devices as a beginner.
- **Professor Messer:** [Troubleshooting hardware (CompTIA A+ 220-1201)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HgeURynWn_w). Builds structured computer diagnostics and an evidence-led workflow.
- **iFixit:** [iPad third-generation battery replacement](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nPgJ-8DTDhA). A device-specific demonstration, not a recommendation to attempt battery repair unsupervised. For tablet and lithium repairs, check **exact model-specific written instructions** and current safety guidance.
- **TronicsFix:** [Repair cases: unusual broken tech](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2WHa4EcDtUI). Observe how symptoms, parts, expected cost and functional proof affect the decision to repair.
- **Louis Rossmann:** [Board-repair training guide and official video channel](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1PkeO_lC5WTPScSV3ZzEEjVuDWeQtL2eHK6jEcf7axA0/htmlpresent). Use for advanced *observation* of component-level diagnosis only.
- **Blancco Technology Group:** [Vendor ITAD data-erasure overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mdgd4VIbLgk). Compare vendor claims with independent NIST guidance; it does not prove a particular erasure method meets your media's requirements.

## Supervisor sign-off rubric

For *every* physical competency:
1. Device or test jig and safe isolation were positively identified.
2. Learner explained hazards, limits and stop-work conditions *before* acting.
3. Evidence was recorded (photos, correct meter range, before/after result).
4. Results were checked rather than accepted on first power-on.
5. Learner can explain what they **would not** attempt and when to refer.

These courses are prerequisites to competency observation, **not** permission to carry out hazardous work, sign off mains repairs, access other people's data, or issue certificates.
