## Mandatory authoring process

For each developing course: produce a topic-specific prerequisite map, two independent sources, three *different* worked cases with analysis, failure mode and safety boundary; add suitable verified demonstration and accessible transcript/audio alternative where they improve comprehension; author realistic counterfactual assessment and externally reviewed evidence rubric. Run the QA scripts and signed-in mobile checks before upgrading the tier. Do not copy the exact same generic troubleshooting lab into unrelated topics.

See [shared rubric](LEARNING-QUALITY-STANDARD.md) for scoring and governance. Last audit is based on source-code presence only; it is not an active HTTP/DRM/paywall availability or independent pedagogy review.
