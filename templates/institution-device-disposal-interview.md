# Institution / business retired-device interview

Keep this to about 10 minutes. Record **role**, not unnecessary personal information.

- Organisation:

- Date:

## Fleet
1. Roughly how many organisation-owned laptops/desktops do you have?
2. Are most devices owned, leased, employee-owned/BYOD, or mixed?
3. Roughly how often are they replaced?
4. About how many laptops/desktops were retired in the last 12 months?
5. What was the size/date of the last disposal batch?

## Current route
6. What usually happens to retired computers?
7. Who currently collects/processes them?
8. Is that decision made locally or by head office/central procurement?
9. Do usable devices get assessed for reuse before recycling?

## Data / evidence
10. Who removes/sanitises data?
11. What evidence do you require: wipe report, serialised certificate, destruction certificate, asset-register update, other?
12. Are devices commonly blocked by MDM/Autopilot/Activation Lock?

## Commercial
13. Do you currently pay for disposal, receive a rebate, or neither?
14. What is the biggest problem with the current process?
15. What would a better regional service need to provide?

## Pilot
16. Would you consider a small formally documented pilot with 5–20 devices?
17. If yes/maybe, what conditions would have to be met?
18. When is the next likely refresh/disposal batch?
