# Customer Terms and Listing Templates

> Draft operational wording, not a substitute for legal advice. Consumer guarantees cannot be excluded.

## Condition statement
“This is a used/refurbished device. Its cosmetic, functional and battery condition are described below. Any known faults specifically identified in this listing form part of the condition disclosed before purchase.”

Avoid blanket “no refunds” or language pretending ACL rights do not exist.

## Returns/support
“If there is a problem, contact us with the order/sale number and a description of the issue. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law are not excluded by this policy.”
