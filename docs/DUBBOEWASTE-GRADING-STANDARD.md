# DubboEwaste Grading Standard

> **PROPOSED DUBBOEWASTE POLICY.** This is an internal standard, not a universal ITAD or legal grading system.

Every listed device receives **three separate grades**.

## Cosmetic grade
**A — Excellent:** very light/no visible wear, no cracks/dents, clean display.  
**B — Good:** ordinary use marks, small scuffs, no structural damage.  
**C — Fair:** obvious wear/dents/scratches but structurally usable; every material defect photographed/disclosed.  
**P — Parts/repair:** structural damage, incomplete or unsuitable for normal retail.

## Functional grade
**F1 — Fully functional:** all required category tests pass.  
**F2 — Functional with disclosed limitation:** e.g. weak battery, cosmetic screen mark, one non-essential port fault.  
**F3 — Repair/parts:** meaningful functional failure or not economical for retail.

## Battery grade
Use measured **full-charge capacity / design capacity** where available:
- **B80+**: ≥80%
- **B60–79**
- **B<60**
- **B?**: reliable measurement unavailable

These bands are **reporting bands**, not claims that 80% is a universal legal/industry threshold. Marketplace programs may impose their own minimums.

## Listing format
Example: **Cosmetic B / Functional F1 / Battery B84**
Then state:
- exact test method/date;
- known defects;
- charger/accessories;
- battery measurement source;
- warranty/returns terms.

## Automatic non-retail conditions
- unresolved ownership or management lock;
- failed sanitisation;
- dangerous battery;
- recall not resolved;
- severe liquid/fire/flood damage unless repaired and independently justified;
- no credible warranty/returns support.
