# Start here — most relevant to Dubbo eWaste

## 1. IT Upgrade – The future of recycling office e-waste
**Podcast:** The Business Of — UNSW Business School  
**Guest:** James Lancaster, founder/CEO of Renew IT  
**Location/context:** Sydney / NSW / Australia  
**Length:** ~27 min  
**Spotify:** https://open.spotify.com/show/6kUw4yMSMNMJQQm4StZBhi  
**Exact episode title:** *IT Upgrade – The future of recycling office e-waste*

### Why this is the single most relevant episode

This is almost the exact business model Dubbo eWaste has converged on.

Lancaster explains that Renew IT originally went into corporations during laptop refreshes, took the old laptops, **sanitised the data, supplied wipe certificates and on-sold the devices**.

He then explains the problem that appears after reuse triage: some equipment, such as printers, has no attractive secondary value and becomes a genuine recycling problem.

Especially relevant parts:

- corporate device refreshes as a supply source;
- ITAD rather than simple "e-waste collection";
- secure data wiping and certificates;
- resale/value recovery;
- deciding what should and should not be resold;
- handling low/no-value material;
- asking recyclers **where equipment physically goes next**;
- advice that small businesses should use an accredited ITAD organisation;
- keeping track of the downstream chain;
- the financial value organisations leave behind when they treat useful IT as waste.

This episode should be listened to **before almost anything else on this list**.

**Verified episode/transcript:**  
https://www.unsw.edu.au/business/our-schools/agsm/about-us/the-business-of-podcast/the-business-of-recycling

# What to listen for, specifically

Do not just listen for broad sustainability ideas. Pull out answers to these Dubbo questions:

### Supply
- Where do they source retired devices?
- Do they target fleet refreshes?
- Minimum viable volume?
- Do businesses pay for pickup or get paid for residual value?

### Triage
- What age/spec/condition survives?
- Whole-device reuse rate?
- Which faults are worth repairing?
- When do they part a machine instead?

### Data
- What sanitisation tool/standard?
- Who gets certificates?
- What happens if wiping fails?

### Resale
- Who buys the refurbished assets?
- Retail, broker, wholesale, charity or original client?
- Who carries warranty/return risk?
- How do they grade devices?

### Social reuse
- Who chooses recipients?
- Who funds refurbishment?
- Does commercial resale cross-subsidise donation?
- What support accompanies the laptop?

### Downstream
- What happens to equipment with zero resale/parts value?
- Can the operator name the downstream recycler?
- What reporting/certification is retained?

### Economics
- What part actually makes money?
- What waste streams lose money?
- What minimum scale is required?
- How much labour is hidden in refurbishment?
- How do they stop low-value stock from accumulating?
