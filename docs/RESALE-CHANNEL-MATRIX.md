# Resale Channel Matrix

| Channel | Best for | Access/gating | Cost model | Warranty/returns | Phase 0 use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| eBay AU | nationally shippable laptops/phones/parts | easy; Refurbished Program is gated | account/plan dependent | ACL + platform rules; Refurb Program adds stronger requirements | **Primary national** |
| Facebook Marketplace local | bulky/local devices | easy | usually local listing/pickup economics | ACL applies if business seller | **Primary local** |
| Gumtree | local/bulky + some shipped deals | easy | current payment pages have changed/conflicted; verify at sale | ACL applies if business seller | **Secondary local** |
| Reebelo | phones/tablets/refurb electronics | professional seller onboarding; second-hand dealer registration warranted in AU terms | contract-specific | marketplace quality/warranty rules | **Later** |
| Back Market AU | professional refurb stock | vetted; quality charter; grade/battery/function requirements | contract-specific | 1-year warranty / 30-day returns on public AU program | **Later** |
| Own site | repeat customers / brand | self-managed | hosting + payment processing | full ACL/admin burden | Later |
| Wholesale/broker | fleets/parts/aged stock | relationship/volume dependent | negotiated | contract | Useful overflow |
| Parts sale | high-value components | channel-specific | fees/shipping | condition disclosure | Strong fallback |
| Scrap | non-viable fractions | downstream rules | payout by kg/category | n/a | Last-value floor |

## Channel decision
- bulky/fragile low-value → local pickup;
- standard business laptop → eBay/local comparison;
- phone/tablet → eBay/local initially, gated refurb marketplace later;
- specialist parts → eBay/enthusiast channels;
- non-viable → parts then published scrap floor.

Never call a device “refurbished” under a platform program unless that platform's eligibility/grade rules are actually met.
