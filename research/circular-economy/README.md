## Evidence labels

- **VERIFIED** — directly supported by a current primary/authoritative source.
- **STRONG INFERENCE** — multiple sources support the conclusion, but the exact fact is not directly published.
- **PARTIAL** — part of the question is answered; an important operational detail remains unknown.
- **UNVERIFIED / DIRECT CONTACT** — web research cannot responsibly answer it.

## Major correction to the original pack

The original pack treated a Whylandra reuse shop as effectively unverified. Later/current Council material closes that gap substantially:

- Council said in October 2024 that staff were preparing business cases for reuse shops at **Whylandra and Wellington**.
- Council's 2024/25 operational-plan response explicitly says both facility master plans include provision for a tip/reuse shop.
- The adopted 2025–35 Waste Strategy includes a 2026/27 action to assess a Whylandra **Resource Recovery Park**, including a community-run **Circular Economy Hub**.
- Current public Whylandra material still describes recycling/disposal rather than an operating reuse shop, so the correct present-tense status is **planned/under assessment, not verified as operating**.

Do not collapse “planned”, “funded”, “contracted”, “built” and “operating” into one status.
