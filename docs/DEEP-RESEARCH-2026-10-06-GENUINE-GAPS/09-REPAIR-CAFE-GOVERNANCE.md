# 09 — Repair Café governance and separation from Dubbo ITAD

**Questions answered:** 27–28  
**Research date:** 6 October 2026

This report addresses governance — deliberately separate from the repo's existing research on what Repair Cafés are and how often they operate.

## 27. What governance structures are realistic?

### Model A — hosted/auspiced by an existing Council/community organisation

**Best first-pilot model.**

Possible host types:

- Council/community venue;
- library-linked program;
- existing incorporated community organisation;
- established repair/community organisation.

Host/auspice can potentially provide:

- venue;
- governance;
- insurance framework;
- volunteer management;
- bank/grant administration;
- risk procedures.

Exact responsibilities must be written down.

### Model B — informal/unincorporated volunteer group

Lowest setup burden, but weak separation for:

- contracts;
- bank account;
- insurance;
- grants;
- asset/tool ownership;
- liability.

Suitable for a very small informal meeting only where the venue/host clearly carries the relevant structure.

### Model C — own NSW incorporated association

NSW Government says an incorporated association:

- is a separate legal entity;
- is designed for small not-for-profit, non-commercial groups;
- needs at least **5 members**;
- needs a constitution;
- needs a public officer;
- can bank/contract/hold property/apply for grants;
- must maintain records and financial reporting;
- needs a management committee of at least 3 members.

Sources:
- https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/incorporated-associations/about-incorporated-associations
- https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/apply-to-register-an-incorporated-association
- https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/incorporated-associations/running-an-incorporated-association

NSW guidance also warns an incorporated association cannot simply be a business providing monetary gain to members/commercial activity.

For one monthly pilot, this is probably excessive administration unless a stable volunteer group emerges.

### Model D — operated inside Dubbo ITAD / Parris Tech Services

**Not recommended for a Council-supported community Repair Café.**

It creates avoidable ambiguity:

- is free volunteer labour benefiting a business?
- are grant-funded tools business assets?
- are broken/unwanted devices being funnelled into commercial stock?
- are customer referrals self-dealing?
- is Council effectively subsidising a private repair funnel?

Keep the projects siblings, not one inside the other.

## Recommended order

1. seek Council/community host or auspice;
2. run 3–6 monthly pilots;
3. form independent incorporated association only if volunteer group/demand justifies it.

## 28. Insurance, volunteering, funding and firewall

### Volunteer governance

Volunteering Australia's National Standards cover:

- leadership/governance;
- risk;
- volunteer records/privacy;
- role definition;
- induction/training;
- partner-agency relationships;
- safe/meaningful participation.

Source:
https://www.volunteeringaustralia.org/get-involved/resources/nationalstandards/

Repair Café International's starter package includes organiser guidance and liability/registration forms.

Source:
https://www.repaircafe.org/en/join/start-your-own/

### Written host agreement should specify

- who is event organiser;
- who provides venue;
- public liability position;
- volunteer personal-accident coverage if any;
- who owns tools;
- incident-report owner;
- lithium/electrical exclusion rules;
- data/privacy procedure;
- child/young-person supervision rules;
- cash/parts handling;
- media/photos consent;
- what happens to unrepairable devices.

### Commercial firewall

**Repair Café must not automatically transfer devices to Dubbo ITAD.**

Rules:

1. owner retains device throughout;
2. no “leave it with Josh and I'll sell/recycle it” default;
3. commercial repair referrals are neutral/non-exclusive;
4. Council-funded tools stay community assets;
5. grants/bank account are separate;
6. volunteer contact list is not a commercial lead list;
7. no Dubbo ITAD sales pitch during diagnosis;
8. if an owner separately wants to transfer a device later, use a new independent transaction/custody record outside the Café;
9. any business donation to the Café is documented sponsorship/donation;
10. conflicts are declared.

### Parts

Default model:

- labour/assistance free;
- owner buys/owns required replacement part;
- volunteer can identify the part;
- Café should avoid becoming a parts retailer in Phase 0.

This reduces money, warranty and stock complexity.

## Recommendation for Dubbo

Pitch to Council:

> a **Council/community-hosted monthly computer repair pilot**, with Dubbo ITAD entirely separate commercially.

If the program develops a volunteer leadership group and regular attendance, revisit incorporation after 6 months.

Do not create a new association merely because a form exists. Governance should solve a real problem.
