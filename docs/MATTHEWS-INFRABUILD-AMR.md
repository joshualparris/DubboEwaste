# Matthews Metal Management, InfraBuild and Australian Metal Recycling — Dubbo research

**Checked:** 2 October 2026 (AEST)  
**Purpose:** establish who currently operates the former Matthews Metal Management yard in Dubbo, what it takes, how it relates to InfraBuild, and whether it could be a downstream outlet or partner for Dubbo eWaste.

## Executive finding

The strongest current evidence says the **34 Mountbatten Drive, Dubbo yard is now operated as Australian Metal Recycling (AMR) by Scrap Metal Services Pty Ltd (ABN 51 640 765 091)**.

However, the history is messy:

- InfraBuild still has a live webpage for **“Matthews Metal Management – Dubbo”** at the exact same address and phone number.
- OneSteel Recycling Pty Limited (ABN 28 002 707 262), an InfraBuild subsidiary, still has **MATTHEWS METAL MANAGEMENT** registered as a business name.
- Australian Metal Recycling’s current website calls Dubbo **“Formerly Matthews Metal Management”** and lists the same yard.
- NSW Police’s Scrap Metal Industry Register dated 19 February 2026 lists **Scrap Metal Services Pty Ltd / Australian Metal Recycling**, registration **SM-0759**, with **34 Mountbatten Drive, Dubbo** as one of its registered scrap-metal yards, managed by Gordon Matthews.
- A March 2025 regional council tender recorded **InfraBuild Recycling (OneSteel Recycling Pty Limited)** and **Scrap Metal Services Pty Ltd** as separate tenderers. That is strong evidence they are separate legal/business operators, not merely two brands of the same company.

**Best interpretation:** the former Matthews regional yards (including Dubbo) have transitioned to the independent Australian Metal Recycling operation run by the Matthews family, while InfraBuild still owns/uses the Matthews Metal Management business name and has some legacy branch pages online. The exact asset-sale/lease/transition agreement and date were not found in public sources.

## 1. Current Dubbo yard

**Address:** 34 Mountbatten Drive, Dubbo NSW 2830  
**Phone:** (02) 6882 1033  
**General phone:** 1300 267 767  
**Scrap Metal Licence/registration:** SM-0759

AMR currently lists Dubbo as one of seven NSW yards. Its contact page labels Dubbo **“Formerly Matthews Metal Management.”**

AMR states that:
- all its yards accept ferrous and non-ferrous metals;
- e-waste is recycled **for free**;
- free drop-off is offered for scrap steel, whitegoods and electrical waste;
- its definition of e-waste includes household appliances, computers, keyboards, monitors, printers, fax machines, copiers and related electrical/electronic equipment containing metal;
- commercial customers are serviced through bins, site consultations and tailored servicing;
- household collection and farm/property clean-up services are offered;
- lithium batteries are listed among materials **not accepted**.

AMR says it pays for aluminium, copper, brass, lead, cars, stainless steel, batteries, radiators and electric motors; it does **not** say it pays for e-waste. Its services page places e-waste in the **free recycling** category.

## 3. Important correction to earlier research

An older Business Recycling directory listing for Matthews stated **no TVs, CRT monitors or printers** were accepted.

That should no longer be treated as current policy because:
- the operator/branding appears to have changed;
- AMR’s current website explicitly includes **printers** in its e-waste definition;
- the old directory is secondary and warns users to confirm details directly.

TV/CRT acceptance remains unresolved.

## 5. InfraBuild / OneSteel relationship

InfraBuild’s recycling business is legally tied to **OneSteel Recycling Pty Limited, ABN 28 002 707 262**.

Evidence:
- The Australian Business Register says OneSteel Recycling Pty Limited is active and has **MATTHEWS METAL MANAGEMENT** as a registered business name from 12 November 2019.
- A July 2026 SIRA notice lists OneSteel Recycling Pty Limited (ABN 28 002 707 262) as a wholly owned employing subsidiary in the Liberty InfraBuild group.
- InfraBuild’s own recycling page says it operates 22 Australian scrap-metal recycling sites and handles about 1.4 million tonnes of recycled metal annually.
- InfraBuild still hosts a branch page titled **Matthews Metal Management – Dubbo**, showing 34 Mountbatten Drive, (02) 6882 1033 and recycling@infrabuild.com.

### Why the InfraBuild Dubbo page looks stale
The current AMR website and the NSW Police register point to AMR/Scrap Metal Services at the Dubbo yard in 2026. The InfraBuild page was indexed from older material and does not explain the later AMR transition.

Do not assume the InfraBuild email address or ownership of the Dubbo yard is current merely because the legacy page remains online.

## 6. Matthews history / return to independent operation

AMR’s own site says John and Gordon Matthews were the original owners of Matthews Metal Management, built the regional business, transitioned it to InfraBuild, and later returned with Australian Metal Recycling.

This is **company-provided history**, not an independently verified sale chronology.

A useful corroborating clue is a March 2025 regional waste tender. It listed:
1. **InfraBuild Recycling (legal name OneSteel Recycling Pty Limited), ABN 28 002 707 262**
2. **Scrap Metal Services Pty Ltd, ABN 51 640 765 091**

as separate bidders for scrap metal, batteries and optional e-waste services.

That strongly supports the conclusion that by March 2025 Scrap Metal Services/AMR and InfraBuild Recycling were separate organisations.

## 8. What this means for Dubbo eWaste

AMR is potentially a much better downstream lead than the earlier research suggested.

### Potential fit
- local Dubbo yard
- current operator says e-waste is accepted free
- commercial customers explicitly supported
- computers/monitors/printers explicitly within its e-waste definition
- accepts metal and non-lithium batteries
- may collect depending on job/volume
- same regional operator family historically involved in Council e-waste processing

### Limits
- AMR is fundamentally a **metal recovery** business, not shown as a refurb/resale partner.
- No evidence found that it will pay for mixed e-waste.
- No evidence found that it provides data destruction suitable for business clients.
- Lithium batteries are explicitly not accepted.
- TVs/CRTs are unresolved.
- No evidence found of current AS/NZS 5377 certification or NTCRS co-regulatory arrangement in the material searched.
- No public evidence found showing the exact downstream recycler/process used for Dubbo e-waste.

### Best proposed relationship
For Dubbo eWaste, treat AMR as a candidate **downstream sink for zero-value metal/electrical material**, not as the primary source of profit.

Ask whether Dubbo eWaste can:
1. deliver sorted, zero-value computer/electrical scrap free;
2. separate batteries first;
3. send printers, monitors and desktops/laptops;
4. send TVs or not;
5. receive a weight docket or recycling receipt;
6. arrange collection once enough volume is accumulated;
7. get a commercial account/bin without minimum monthly volume.

## 9. Exact call script

Call **(02) 6882 1033**:

> Hi, I’m setting up a very small electronics refurbishment and reuse operation in Dubbo. I’ll keep and resell anything repairable, but I need a legitimate downstream outlet for the zero-value remainder.
>
> I saw Australian Metal Recycling lists e-waste as free to recycle and the Dubbo yard at 34 Mountbatten Drive. Can I bring small commercial loads of sorted computers and electrical gear to you?
>
> Specifically, do you accept:
> - laptops and desktops after data drives are removed/wiped
> - monitors
> - printers
> - phones and tablets
> - flat-screen TVs
> - CRT TVs/monitors
> - loose circuit boards
> - power supplies/cables
>
> Do lithium batteries need to be removed first? Is there any fee, minimum quantity, or business-account requirement? Can you provide a weight docket or recycling receipt, and at what volume would you collect from me instead?

Then ask:

> Are you now Australian Metal Recycling rather than InfraBuild/Matthews Metal Management at this yard? I’m seeing both names online and want to make sure I record the current legal operator correctly.
