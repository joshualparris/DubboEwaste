Safe optimisation policy

Measure, change the bottleneck, retest

Keep Defender, Windows Update and system-managed paging. Prefer targeted startup cleanup, sensible indexing exclusions, Storage Sense without deleting Downloads, normal SSD TRIM/HDD optimisation, Game Mode, trusted drivers and maintenance outside LAN events. Avoid destructive debloat scripts, permanent Update shutdown, pagefile removal, HPET folklore and mass service disabling.
