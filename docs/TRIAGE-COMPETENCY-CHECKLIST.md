# Triage competency checklist

This is a practice and assessment record, not a qualification or legal authority. The operator should be able to demonstrate each skill on known, non-sensitive devices before accepting public or customer-owned equipment.

## Gate

`PASS` requires a safe, repeatable, documented result. `HOLD` means stop and seek advice or a known route. `FAIL` means do not accept that category until retrained and reassessed.

|---|---|---|

## Reassessment triggers

- new device category or media type;
- any safety incident or near miss;
- failed wipe, account lock or ownership dispute;
- material change to tools, premises, insurer or downstream route;
- repeated returns, misclassification or rejected loads.
