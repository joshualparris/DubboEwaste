Learn to troubleshoot with a multimeter

Six video lessons for safe low-voltage testing of laptop chargers, laptop power circuits and desktop power supplies. Start with the basics, then move to practical diagnosis.

Workshop equipment: SCA Hobbyist Digital Multimeter — collected 8 October 2026.

Language verification status: English words are visible in the subtitles shown in your screenshot of the first beginner video; I did not independently listen to the narration. The spoken language of the remaining five videos has not been confirmed from audio or caption tracks. Their English titles are not proof of English narration. Videos below are labelled accordingly.
Safety first: Begin with low-voltage DC and disconnected-cable continuity checks only. Never open laptop chargers or desktop power supplies. Never measure continuity on a live circuit. Red probe goes to V/Ω, black to COM, when measuring voltage. Verify connector pinouts before testing.
