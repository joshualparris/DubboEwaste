# First 30 Device Experiment

## Hypotheses
1. ≥50% of carefully pre-screened intake can reach whole-device reuse.
2. Median total hands-on labour for straightforward business laptops can stay under 90 minutes.
3. Net profit remains positive after valuing labour.
4. Return/fault rate stays materially below 10%.
5. At least two sources provide repeatable supply.
6. Downstream cost for rejects does not erase recovered value.

## Sample
First **30 accepted devices**, while also recording every rejected offer.

## Measure
- source;
- category/model;
- accept/reject reason;
- support/lock state;
- intake minutes;
- sanitisation result;
- repair minutes/parts;
- route;
- list/sale price;
- fees/shipping;
- days in stock;
- return;
- net profit;
- profit/hour.

## Stop conditions
Pause a category if:
- safety incident;
- repeated unresolved ownership/lock issue;
- 3 consecutive uneconomic units of same type;
- return/fault rate >15% after at least 10 sold units;
- no lawful downstream route;
- storage exceeds defined capacity.

## Review
At devices 10, 20 and 30:
- tighten acceptance rules;
- update max acquisition prices;
- remove low-value categories;
- identify repeat sources.

## Success gate after 30
Expand only if:
- legal/planning/insurance gates are closed;
- positive net profit after labour;
- acceptable returns;
- storage controlled;
- at least one repeat supply channel;
- downstream documentation works.
