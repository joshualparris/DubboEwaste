## High-value corrections discovered

1. **eBay:** the old roughly-13% Phase 0 fee assumption is wrong. Eligible Australian sellers with no Pro plan and no more than AU$25,000 eBay sales over the prior 12 months currently pay no transaction/final-value fee. Other optional/applicable costs still matter.
2. **Windows 10:** Windows 11 remains the preferred target, but Microsoft now offers Australian consumer ESU through **12 October 2027**, so a transparently disclosed temporary supported Windows 10 path can exist.
3. **Repair jobs:** customer-owned data-bearing devices need the current repair/data-loss notice before acceptance; refurbished-goods/parts use triggers prescribed notice wording.
4. **NIST:** SP 800-88 **Rev. 2** (September 2025) is current.
5. **WHS:** from **1 July 2026**, NSW says Codes of Practice are minimum expected performance standards under the WHS Act.
6. **Insurance:** the old $800–$1,500 annual estimate is not defensible for the exact activity. Use an actual quote.
7. **Battery safety:** a generic metal battery bin is not a complete control. Phase 0 should reject loose/damaged lithium and use risk-assessed controls.
8. **GST:** the **$75,000** registration threshold is now directly verified from ATO.
9. **Business name:** 2026–27 fee confirmed at **$47/1 year or $108/3 years**.
10. **Painted Brush:** the current business deliberately moved **online-only from 1 February 2026**, showing that successful microbusiness scaling can also reverse when lower overhead better suits the business.
