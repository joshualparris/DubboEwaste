# Ziperase

Checked: 2026-10-02 · Evidence: EVD-BENCH-009 · Role: Erasure

## Publicly advertised features

Array Erase processes large drive batches across multiple interfaces, with hardware-dependent hot swap, central control, SMART health and reports. Quick-start documentation describes offline operation, extra fields and PDF branding. Product family includes Mac/Chromebook workflows, Autopilot detection and independent Verify.

## Limits and unresolved details

Hundreds of drives is an advertised scale, not a benchmark run here. Confirm SAN topology, secure-erase coverage and certificate validation.

## AssetFlow implications

Use this product as a benchmark in these domains: DATA,DIAG,INT. Build operational records and release controls in AssetFlow; use documented adapters for external device processing. Do not label a browser workflow as equivalent to its erasure engine, certification or physical automation.
