# How to open a Dubbo ITAD branch / partner hub — cost, effort, payoff and exact process

**Research date:** 2 October 2026 (AEST)  
**Scope:** Greenbox, G1 Asset Management, Fliptech, WV Technologies, Renew IT, Shred-X, and Iron Mountain / former ACT Logistics.

## Executive answer

The most important finding is:

> **None of the seven publicly offers a simple "buy a Dubbo franchise" product.**

There are three genuinely different ways this could happen:

1. **Partner / white-label model** — you own the local customer relationship, while the national ITAD company performs some or all secure processing.
2. **Approved satellite / aggregation hub** — you operate a secure Dubbo facility under the parent company's standards.
3. **Corporate branch** — the parent company itself opens the branch, owns the systems/contracts and employs or contracts local staff.

The cheapest and lowest-effort route is **not** a warehouse.

The strongest sequence is:

**partner first → prove recurring clients → run regional jobs → measure volume and margin → open local secure premises only when the economics justify them.**

# 2. Critical correction — "branch" does not necessarily mean you own it

For several candidates, a Dubbo "branch" would probably mean:

- the parent company signs the lease;
- the parent company owns the security/compliance systems;
- the parent company holds certifications;
- you are local manager / employee / contractor / business-development partner.

That means the local operator's upside might be:

- salary;
- commission;
- service margin;
- subcontracting revenue;

rather than owning the entire resale margin.

For **Greenbox and G1**, the public model gives a much clearer route for an independent local business to earn revenue without waiting for a corporate-owned branch.

## Labour floor if hiring staff

From 1 July 2026:

- National Minimum Wage: **$26.44/hour**
- **$1,004.90/week**
- casual floor for an award/agreement-free adult: **$33.05/hour**
- super guarantee: **12%**

Sources:
- https://www.fairwork.gov.au/pay-and-wages/minimum-wages
- https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/super-guarantee

### One full-time worker — absolute national-minimum floor

Base:
- $1,004.90 × 52 = **$52,254.80/year**

12% super:
- **$6,270.58**

Minimum wage + super:
- **$58,525.38/year**

This excludes:
- workers compensation;
- payroll software;
- leave-related on-costs beyond base calculation;
- allowances;
- overtime;
- award rates that may actually apply.

Therefore it is only a legal wage-floor benchmark.

### One casual for two 8-hour days each week

At $33.05/hour:
- 16 hours × 52 × $33.05 = **$27,497.60**
- 12% super = **$3,299.71**
- total = **$30,797.31/year**

Again, an applicable award could require more.

# 5. Three realistic cost levels

## Level A — partner / white-label sales operation

You do **not** lease a warehouse.

You:
- find Dubbo/Central West clients;
- qualify the jobs;
- sell/white-label the national provider's ITAD service;
- parent provider performs secure collection/processing;
- you earn agreed margin / commission / service revenue.

### Published hard-cost floor

Potentially:
- company partner fee: **no public fee stated** for Greenbox/G1/WV/Iron Mountain;
- new business name if required: **$47/year**;
- premises: **$0**;
- Blancco: **$0** if the parent performs erasure;
- branch vehicle: **$0** if parent collects.

### Unknown but real costs
- public/product liability;
- professional/cyber cover if required;
- phone/software;
- sales travel;
- company-specific commercial terms.

This is the **lowest-cost path**.

---

## Level B — secure Dubbo collection / aggregation hub

Example first-year baseline using current public benchmarks:

- 190 m² rent: **$20,540 + GST/year**
- low-end commercial security allowance: **$5,000**
- Blancco 50-device bundle benchmark: **$982.30** if local erase is allowed
- second-hand dealer licence: **$692** if legally required
- business name: **$47** if needed

### Baseline subtotal

**$27,261.30** before:

- GST cashflow;
- lease bond;
- outgoings;
- insurance;
- electricity/internet;
- shelving/racking;
- lockable data-media cabinet;
- fire/battery controls;
- vehicle/freight;
- tools/test benches;
- computers;
- signage;
- legal/accounting;
- planning/DA if required;
- labour.

This is **not an exact total**. It is the minimum modelled first-year baseline from currently published figures.

### With one full-time worker at national-minimum wage floor

$27,261.30
+ $58,525.38 wage/super floor
= **$85,786.68**

before all the other unknown items above.

That demonstrates why a local warehouse should come **after** the partner model proves the volume.

---

## Level C — full processing branch

A true secure ITAD processing facility may require:

- secure premise;
- CCTV;
- access control;
- alarm monitoring;
- restricted data zone;
- chain-of-custody system;
- processing benches;
- diagnostic stations;
- data-erasure licensing;
- lockable media storage;
- verified destruction route;
- racking;
- battery/fire separation;
- loading equipment;
- staff;
- vehicle/freight;
- certifications/audits depending on parent model.

There is **no defensible exact public cost** for this because each parent company defines its facility standard privately.

Do not sign a warehouse lease until the parent supplies a written facility specification.

# 21. Highest-return sequence before spending on premises

## Step 1 — obtain three commercial documents

1. **Greenbox Partner Programme pricing / rulebook**
2. **G1 white-label pricing / commercial terms**
3. **Fliptech written pilot / regional operator terms**

These three documents answer most of the currently unknowable questions:
- margin;
- buyback split;
- client ownership;
- asset ownership;
- insurance;
- reporting;
- local custody;
- local sanitisation;
- use of brand;
- territory.

## Step 2 — compare real dollars

Use the same sample job for all three:

> 100 business-class laptops, 3–5 years old, collected in Dubbo, serialised, sanitised, tested and routed to resale/reuse/recycling.

Ask each provider for:
- client charge;
- buyback/rebate;
- your margin;
- freight;
- erase cost;
- reporting cost;
- failed-device cost;
- who receives resale upside.

## Step 3 — only then decide whether a local hub makes sense

If the parent will collect directly and still leave enough margin locally, a warehouse may never be necessary.

If the biggest cost is repeated Sydney/Canberra transport, a Dubbo secure hub becomes more valuable.

# 22. What "highest payoff" currently means

Because no provider publishes local partner margin, the actual dollar winner is unknown.

The current evidence supports:

### Highest likely payoff per dollar invested
**Greenbox Partner Program**

because it combines:
- direct partner status;
- white label/co-branding;
- direct client servicing;
- preferred pricing;
- explicit asset-recovery margins;
- no public franchise fee;
- no warehouse requirement to start.

### Lowest operational effort
**G1 white-label**

because G1 can perform:
- collection;
- data destruction;
- processing;
- reporting;

while the local IT provider owns the customer-facing service.

**Fliptech**

because a Dubbo model could potentially be negotiated as a new regional operating arrangement rather than joining a mature predefined channel.

**Iron Mountain / WV / Greenbox**

but these may give the local operator less ownership/autonomy.
