# Canonical Podcast List — DubboEwaste

**Updated:** 2 Oct 2026  
**Purpose:** one deduplicated listening list. The older podcast files remain historical research but this is the canonical starting point.

## Start here — Australia / NSW first

1. **Renew IT — James Lancaster, All Things Circular EP49**  
   Spotify: https://open.spotify.com/episode/6WyTlDuqgcYpBieTiEsdB2  
   Why: NSW operator; corporate refresh → sanitise → refurbish/resell → downstream recovery.

2. **FlipTech — Austin Turpin, Life on Planet A #82**  
   Spotify show: https://open.spotify.com/show/2m7vWs5BoO9s1iU1HdsOcc  
   Why: closest NSW founder/operator comparison; startup story and e-waste opportunity.

3. **UNSW Business School — IT Upgrade: The future of recycling office e-waste**  
   Spotify show: https://open.spotify.com/show/6kUw4yMSMNMJQQm4StZBhi  
   Why: Renew IT / office e-waste / Australian commercial pathway.

4. **UTS / 2SER — Is E-waste really waste?**  
   Spotify show: https://open.spotify.com/show/44ritbasQXGivQQDX4wdrM  
   Why: NSW circular-economy framing and reuse-before-recycling.

5. **SK tes Australia — How LEAP Creates Circular Jobs in Australia**  
   Spotify: https://open.spotify.com/episode/6crKcAJ6pcgdxeroWbyH2p  
   Why: electronics lifecycle plus Australian social-enterprise/jobs angle.

6. **WorkVentures / digital poverty — Caroline McDaid**  
   Spotify show: https://open.spotify.com/show/570Gk9iXj52l71aqJMdrnL  
   Why: refurb redistribution and digital inclusion.

7. **Good360 — Susan Wallis / Community Matters**  
   Spotify show: https://open.spotify.com/show/3LQnIRqiRXOmpMB67YulsK  
   Why: corporate surplus → charities/schools → community distribution.

8. **Veena Sahajwalla — Trash Talking: Recycling**  
   Spotify: https://open.spotify.com/episode/7rworfp9mDP97LUqDcqWM9  
   Why: NSW downstream materials/microfactory context.

9. **Operational ITAM — Hardware Asset Management: The Seven Gates**  
   Spotify: https://open.spotify.com/episode/0METUyopE9s2yYMN6zpa6Q

10. **All Things Circular EP22 — ITAD, E-Waste, Recycling**  
    Spotify: https://open.spotify.com/episode/1wErId7YMtNhuO1GU4Rlc1  
    Why: direct ITAD and asset-recovery mechanics.

11. **Ticket Volume — IT Recycling done right**  
    Spotify show: https://open.spotify.com/show/0gWhc3wCJEqq30JXLqwU6w  
    Why: data security, responsible recycler selection and asset recovery.

12. **Eco Sheroes — Is Your Phone's Data Safe When You Recycle It?**  
    Spotify show: https://open.spotify.com/show/0ZYeGIT5uL9WotjuovC3J1  
    Why: grading, data wiping, repair economics and second-hand electronics.

## Repair / pilot economics

13. **Profit or Loss — Joey Does Tech**  
    Spotify show: https://open.spotify.com/show/033Tk9GjGbPG3A2Jqf1gg7

14. **Add To Cart — How to Prove Demand Before You Buy the Stock #659**  
    Spotify show: https://open.spotify.com/show/1aTisBSeeV4y6ZQ0cslknG
