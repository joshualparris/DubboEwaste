# Open Evidence Requests — What Still Requires Direct Contact

Date: 8 October 2026

Web research has reached the point where the biggest unknowns are **not responsibly answerable from public pages**.

## Priority A — Dubbo Regional Council

Ask Resource Recovery & Efficiency / Procurement:

### Whylandra master plan and reuse
1. Has the Whylandra Waste Facility Master Plan now been adopted?
2. Can Council provide the current master plan or reuse-shop concept drawings?
3. Is the Whylandra reuse shop/Circular Economy Hub funded?
4. What delivery year is currently targeted?
5. Will electronics be in scope?
6. Would items be selected before or after formal acceptance as Council waste?
7. At exactly what point does title/ownership of dropped material transfer?
8. What policy or licence prohibits/allows pre-disposal diversion by a contracted reuse operator?
9. Is Council considering an external/social-enterprise operator?

### E-waste
10. Who holds the e-waste collection/processing contract after the Matthews Metals contract expired on 30 June 2025?
11. What is the contract/reference number and term?
12. What facility receives material first?
13. What downstream processors receive:
    - computers/laptops;
    - boards;
    - drives;
    - metals;
    - plastics?
14. Does the contract allow functional-device reuse?
15. What data-destruction standard/report is required?
16. Can Council provide annual e-waste tonnes by facility?

### Textiles / tyres
17. Who is the current textile recycler?
18. Where is material sorted/processed?
19. What proportion is reused versus fibre/rag/downcycling/export?
20. Who holds the post-June-2025 tyre recycling arrangement?
21. What downstream facility/process is used?

## Priority B — current/former e-waste processor

Ask AMR / relevant awarded contractor:

1. Do you currently receive Dubbo Regional Council e-waste?
2. Which categories?
3. Do you reuse/resell functional equipment, or material-process all units?
4. How are data-bearing devices handled?
5. Can customers receive device-level sanitisation/destruction evidence?
6. What downstream facilities receive each commodity?
7. What are average material/reuse recovery rates?
8. Would you collaborate with a pre-processing refurbisher so reusable equipment is removed before material recycling?

## Priority C — charity shops

For each Dubbo op shop, collect a yes/no matrix:

| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| Electrical goods accepted? | Defines reuse route |
| Whitegoods accepted? | Bulky repair/reuse |
| Furniture accepted? | Diversion pathway |
| Test/tag performed? | Electrical safety |
| Repairs performed? | True reuse capacity |
| Pickup offered? | Transport barrier |
| Unsold textiles go where? | Downstream traceability |
| Unsold hardgoods go where? | Leakage to landfill/recycling |
| E-waste recycler used? | Downstream chain |
| Would accept referrals from a repair/reuse hub? | Ecosystem design |

## Priority D — Men's Sheds

Ask both Dubbo sheds:

1. Do members repair electrical/electronic items?
2. Any retired electronics/computer technicians?
3. Are members of the public able to bring repair jobs?
4. Could women/non-members attend a hosted community Repair Café event if arranged with a partner organisation?
5. What public-liability cover applies?
6. Can lithium-battery devices be brought on site?
7. What test/tag/electrical-work restrictions apply?
8. Would the shed host one trial “fix your old laptop/device” table?

## Priority E — local repair businesses

Ask representative repairers:

1. What do you decline most often?
2. What makes a repair uneconomic?
3. What happens to abandoned/unrepairable devices?
4. Do you harvest parts?
5. Would you accept triaged referrals?
6. Would you volunteer/contract for periodic community repair sessions?
7. Would you refer reusable retired computers to a local ITAD/refurb program?

## GIPA candidate if ordinary contact cannot close the gaps

Request only the specific records needed, not a fishing expedition:

> Current contract/award and scope for collection and processing of e-waste from Dubbo Regional Council waste facilities after 30 June 2025, including contractor name, contract term, service specification, permitted reuse/recovery requirements, data-bearing asset requirements, and named first downstream receiving facility/facilities.

Optional second request:

> Current adopted/final Whylandra Waste Facility Master Plan and the portions addressing reuse shop, resource recovery park or circular economy hub infrastructure, including implementation staging and approved funding where publicly releasable.

## Evidence discipline

A phone answer should be recorded as:
- person/role;
- organisation;
- date/time;
- exact question;
- summary of answer;
- whether documentary confirmation was offered.

Do not convert “someone told us” into a VERIFIED claim unless the evidence register records the source.
