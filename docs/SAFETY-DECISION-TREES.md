# Safety Decision Trees

## Lithium battery

### At intake
**Is the battery/device swollen, leaking, unusually hot, smoking, punctured/crushed, fire/flood affected or recalled?**
- YES → do not charge/test. Reject at intake where safe to do so, or isolate the unexpected discovery and follow current FRNSW/B-cycle/accredited-collector guidance. Smoke/fire/off-gassing/immediate danger → evacuate and call 000.
- NO → continue normal intake.

### Charging
- compatible compliant charger only;
- supervised;
- non-combustible test area;
- no overnight/unattended charging;
- stop on heat, swelling, odour, noise or abnormal behaviour.

### Shipping
Never rely on a single generic lithium rule. Check the exact carrier + service + battery configuration against `docs/AU-SHIPPING-MATRIX.md`.

## Electrical safety

### Device powered only by SELV/low-voltage external adapter
- inspect adapter/lead for damage, counterfeit/unknown markings, wrong voltage/polarity;
- do not repair mains side unless competent/licensed as required;
- replace suspect adapter with compliant known-good unit.

### Detachable 230 V lead
- inspect plug, insulation, strain relief and earth where applicable;
- damaged/modified lead → do not sell with it; replace with compliant lead;
- unresolved NSW second-hand electrical testing/labelling requirement → follow conservative policy and seek written regulator answer.

### Internal mains equipment (desktop PSU, monitor, TV, printer, UPS)
- damaged casing/lead/plug, exposed conductors, liquid ingress or burnt smell → stop;
- do not open/repair mains section unless work is legally permitted and within competence;
- use qualified service where required.

### Unknown
If unsure whether work is licensed electrical work, **do not improvise**. Stop and refer.
