## Executive findings

1. AMR's published mission and flexible commercial services support the fit of a reuse-first partnership. It advertises seven NSW locations and e-waste intake; no verified public AMR computer refurbishment partnership or specific authorisation for Dubbo has been found. Its founders' public commitment to regional sustainability is encouraging, not approval.
2. **New hard licensing gate:** Blancco's current published EULA section 3.2 restricts service-provider licences to the licensee/direct employees, designated facility and permitted scope; third-party partners, redistribution and customer-site use are restricted. A separate licence or specifically authorised arrangement is needed unless the actual controlling order/agreement permits the proposed model. Do not share credentials or credits.
3. **New current legal evidence:** NTCRS Rules compilation 3, 1 July 2026, sections 4, 7 and 16, require comprehensive recycling and 90% material recovery by weight in the scheme and facility-specific AS/NZS 5377 certification for recycling. The 2014 reporting methodology explicitly excludes whole-device reuse/refurbishment from material recovery. This is a possible incentive/contract conflict, not proof of AMR's present funding or a universal prohibition on reuse.
4. **New council evidence:** current 20 November 2026 GIPA contracts register, single page, checked text and page image; no Matthews or e-waste entry found. Historical June 2023–June 2025 award still does not prove the current arrangement. The register covers reportable contracts, not every arrangement.
5. National Device Reuse Principles agreed 4 September 2026 and published on Finance's website dated 25 November; apply to surplus government ICT, explicitly preserve disposal/security/procurement obligations. Tech Reuse for Good charter invites ITAD/refurbishment, logistics and device redirection and annual reporting. Its playbook includes permitted resale to fund reuse, not blanket authority to sell donated or contracted assets.
6. AssetFlow's current source contains existing Council pilot gates and text report import; Blancco signed XML authenticity validation is not demonstrated. A hash of a file is not signature verification. Do not build another asset platform or repurpose donation-only Council batch as commercial intake.

## Economics sensitivity (planning assumptions, not quotes)

Model deliberately uses transparent invented planning values rather than reporting any AMR or vendor price. AUD, consistent pre-GST basis; actual GST treatment depends on registration/accountant. ATO [registration](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/registering-for-gst) threshold generally $75k business / $150k qualifying NFP, not automatic NFP status. Each input: $5 acquisition, $12 assumed media erasure cost, 30 min intake labour at $30/hr; fixed $50 transport/consumables per batch; each usable device $40 parts + 45 min refurb labour; rejects $5 residual handling. Assume one medium/input for costing, $150 average net sales receipt if sale authorised, no warranty reserve/insurance/fees/tax or extra failure/repair hours included. Formula cost=50+37n+57.5k, cash before labour=cost-15n-22.5k. n=inputs, k=released. Donation receives zero sales; donor funding must cover cost. Estimates are not valuations; use actual model mix, quotes and timed work. Do not interpret positive contribution as net profit.

## Sanitisation decisions

| Medium | Proposed control |
| --- | --- |
| SATA/SAS HDD | Inventory serial/condition, authorised Clear or Purge under owner's policy using approved technique; verify and independently validate; if unavailable or policy mandates, destruction |
| SATA/NVMe SSD | Prefer approved device-specific Purge method when policy requires; firmware capability/implementation and key lifecycle must be validated; ordinary overwrite does not assure inaccessible/spare cells |
| Embedded eMMC/flash or hybrid | Inspect every medium; vendor-supported approved method for all storage; otherwise hold/authorised destruction; do not treat no removable drive as no media |
| Failed, frozen, inaccessible, locked or unknown media | Quarantine, never browse files or bypass locks; authorised return or destruction, matching serial and proof; hold entire device pending clearance |
| No media | Physically inspect, record no-media state and reviewer; no wipe report invented |

Signed external original report + serial + approved method + version + verification + validation + custody chain required. A PDF alone or an AssetFlow file hash does not prove vendor signature authenticity. Separate ordinary reuse from sensitive/classified or mandated destruction workflows.

## Pilot heads of agreement (draft for review; not a contract)

- Parties: authorised AMR entity and independent operator; identify legal entity, authorised signatories, approved location, permitted source and title transfer terms; no access to general yard stock.
- Stage 0: preferably 5–10 verified drive-free eligible devices first; scale to maximum 20 only after documented data and custody acceptance. No public drop-off, unattended charging, damaged batteries, general waste, or employer premises/equipment.
- AMR verifies stream and title, authorises selected batch, records manifest and defines residual acceptance. Operator records unique asset/media IDs, condition, transfer acknowledgements, custody events, holds, repair/functional/electrical/recall checks and authorised release outcome. Owner approves reuse purpose, destruction standard and reporting as applicable.
- Written agreement covers fee/ownership/resale/donation, site safety, transport, subcontractors, data retention/privacy, breach notification, insurance, liability, approval of publicity, reconciliation, audit and termination. No unilateral waiver of statutory rights. Failed media go to a verified destruction provider; confirm exact provider and battery acceptance separately.
- RACI: AMR authorised signatory accountable for stock release; operator accountable for custody and refurbishment; licensed erasure provider responsible for approved erasure and authentic evidence; independent reviewer validates release; insurer/site owner/regulators confirm their own scopes; downstream provider accountable for accepted residuals and documented disposal. Council/co-regulator consulted/approves where applicable.
- STOP on title discrepancy, missing drive, unreadable report, signature/serial mismatch, unauthorised user, battery hazard, theft/breach, expired approval/insurance, stock accumulation or unconfirmed downstream acceptance. Report and reconcile before resuming.
- Metrics: devices received/eligible/reused/donated/sold/recycled/held, actual mass by disposition, media passed/failed/destroyed, turnaround, cash and labour, recipient acceptance. Avoid equating diverted from shredding with diverted from landfill; no emissions savings without defensible baseline/LCA. At 20 devices require all assets/media reconciled and zero unverified releases; compare reuse yield and costs against measured baseline.
