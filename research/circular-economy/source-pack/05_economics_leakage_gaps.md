## 19. Repair economics (general evidence, national/international)
- Nothing Dubbo-specific yet [UNVERIFIED].
- Likely drivers: labour rates vs replacement price; sealed devices; proprietary parts/software locks; freight; liability for electrical safety; data privacy; shortage of technicians (INFERENCE — validate with local repairer interviews).
- Distinguish **cannot be repaired** (no parts/lock/damage) from **not worth repairing** (labour > value). Most low-cost consumer items fall in the second group.
- Repair Cafés avoid labour economics by using volunteer time; Adelaide council consultation cites ~80% success rate for existing cafes [VERIFIED, SA context].
