## Note-taking template

For every episode watched or heard, record:

- episode/title and date accessed;
- operator/process described;
- front-door acceptance threshold;
- lock/data/battery handling;
- repair versus parts decision;
- downstream/residual route;
- claim that is directly sourced versus inference;
- one change, if any, to the Dubbo Phase 0 SOP.

Do not treat a podcast claim as a current legal, safety, price or partnership fact without checking the primary source.
