# Australian Community / Trading Rules

## Overclockers Australia (OCAU)
A long-standing OCAU FAQ states that Trading Forums access requires membership for **more than 90 days**. A 2024 discussion repeats the same rule.

Sources:
- https://forums.overclockers.com.au/threads/mega-sticky-and-faq.118215/
- https://forums.overclockers.com.au/threads/whats-the-conditions-for-getting-privileges-to-sell.1341616/

I did **not** verify a current “100 posts” requirement. Do not repeat it.

Older OCAU rules also say commercial advertising belongs in the appropriate Trading Post context and excessive business promotion can lead to account cancellation. Check current forum rules before using it commercially.

## Whirlpool
Whirlpool is primarily a discussion forum rather than a general resale marketplace. Use it for Australian technical/provider knowledge and community research, not as a default commercial sales channel unless current forum rules explicitly permit the activity.

## Reddit
Useful communities include global repair/flipping/electronics groups, but rules vary by subreddit and change. Treat Reddit as experience-sharing evidence, not authoritative law/pricing data.

## Policy
Before posting commercial offers:
1. read current forum/subreddit rules;
2. disclose business status where required;
3. do not evade minimum-membership or anti-spam rules;
4. never use a community's reputation as proof a seller/buyer is safe.
