## One new, useful finding: Fair Trading grants *conditional* exemptions

NSW Fair Trading publishes a list of licensed dealers holding **limited exemptions** (15 listed as at 27 Feb 2025). They fall into three types:
1. **Identity verification** (relief from documentary ID evidence),
2. **Record-keeping / police notification** (relief from electronic records or notification),
3. **Retention period** (relief from the **14-day hold**).

Holders include carrier **trade-in and upgrade programs** (Telstra Trade-In, "Upgrade and Protect", "Swap & Go", Optus programs). Source: https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/running-a-business/industry-specific-business-requirements/pawnbrokers-and-second-hand-dealers/limited-exemptions-for-pawnbrokers-and-second-hand-dealers **[Primary]**

**Why this matters for Dubbo eWaste [Derived]:** the repo's biggest workflow risk is that, *if* a licence is required, the **14-day "no alteration" hold could block data wiping**. The exemption list shows Fair Trading **has granted retention-period relief** for structured device trade-in programs. So the Fair Trading question can be framed as a two-part ask:
1. Does the recycling-program exemption apply? If yes, no licence is needed.
2. If not, would Fair Trading consider a **limited exemption from the 14-day hold for data-sanitisation steps only**, on the basis that wiping is a security requirement and the device's identity (serial/IMEI) and records are preserved, as it has done for carrier trade-in programs?

Add this as a follow-up question to `CALL-SCRIPTS.md` §1 / `EMAIL-DRAFTS.md` (Fair Trading) when those files are next edited. They weren't edited here, to avoid conflicting with other agents.
