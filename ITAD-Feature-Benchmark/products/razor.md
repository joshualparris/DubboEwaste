# RazorERP

Checked: 2026-10-02 · Evidence: EVD-BENCH-002 · Role: ERP

## Publicly advertised features

Cloud inventory and multichannel synchronisation; chain-of-custody recycling workflows; warehouse locations; localisation and multiple currencies. Official mobile apps advertise receiving, scanning, bin transfers, photos and label printing. Vendor help documents describe settlements and fair-market-value/devaluation templates.

## Limits and unresolved details

Razor and MAKOR are separate products. Confirm exact marketplace adapters, permissions and settlement calculations in a demo.

## AssetFlow implications

Use this product as a benchmark in these domains: INV,LOG,SALE,FIN,INT. Build operational records and release controls in AssetFlow; use documented adapters for external device processing. Do not label a browser workflow as equivalent to its erasure engine, certification or physical automation.
