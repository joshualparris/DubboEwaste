**Subject:** Does the recycling-program exemption (Regulation 2026 cl 33) cover a small electronics reuse program in Dubbo?

Hello,

I run [Dubbo eWaste / entity name, ABN], a small reuse-first electronics program in Dubbo, NSW. I'm asking for Fair Trading's written view on whether the Pawnbrokers and Second-hand Dealers Act 1996 requires me to hold a second-hand dealer licence for the activities below, or whether clause 33 of the Pawnbrokers and Second-hand Dealers Regulation 2026 applies.

**What I do**

- I receive unwanted computers, laptops, phones and tablets free of charge from local businesses and households. [Describe how the public and businesses learn about it, and where items are received.]
- I record each item, wipe data, test and repair what can be reused, and [give away / sell] working devices. [Describe channels: in person, Facebook Marketplace, eBay; typical price range; roughly how many a month.]
- I give items that can't be reused to [named recyclers]. [Say whether any are paid for.]
- I [do / do not] buy devices or parts. [If yes: from whom.]
- I also repair customers' own devices for a fee. Those devices aren't bought or sold.

**Questions**

1. Does clause 33(a) ("buying or selling goods collected in a recycling program or rubbish collection program") cover selling devices collected free through a program like this after they've been wiped, repaired or refurbished?
2. What does Fair Trading consider a "recycling program" or "rubbish collection program" for clause 33? For example: must it be public, non-profit, run by a particular kind of entity, or registered anywhere?
3. Does reuse or refurbishment count as "recycling" for this clause, or only material recovery?
4. Are parts harvested from collected devices still "goods collected in" the program when sold separately?
5. If I also buy some devices or parts, does that take the whole operation outside clause 33, or only the bought items?
6. Does it matter whether sales are online (eBay, Facebook Marketplace) or in person, or how often they happen (given section 38)?
7. If I donate refurbished devices to a charity that sells them in a fundraising appeal, does section 4(2)(a) cover the charity's sales, and does my role need anything beyond the charity's written participant authority? Does an appeal by a fundraiser that is exempt from holding an authority count as "authorised" for section 4(2)(a)?
8. For goods bought from a NSW government agency or State owned corporation, does the government-purchase exemption also cover reselling them?
9. If clause 33 doesn't apply: can a prospective operator apply under section 4A for an exemption from section 7, or only from specific duties? What's the fee, form and timeframe? If a licence is required, would Fair Trading consider a limited exemption from the 14-day no-alteration rule (s 21) for serial- or IMEI-tracked devices, so data can be wiped promptly?
9a. Can the licence fee be waived, reduced or postponed under clause 32 of the 2026 Regulation, and what evidence is needed? Can that be decided before paying?
10. Is there a process for a formal written determination, and how long does a reply usually take?

Thank you. I'm happy to provide more detail or a copy of our intake and records process.
