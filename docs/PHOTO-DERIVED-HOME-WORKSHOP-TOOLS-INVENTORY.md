## Evidence and confidence rule

This is an **observed inventory**, not a purchase wishlist.

- **Confirmed** = clearly visible in a supplied photo or explicitly purchased/identified in the same conversation.
- **Likely** = visible enough to identify the category but not the exact model.
- **Historical** = previously photographed or identified, but current possession/location has not been re-confirmed.
- Exact counts are only used when visually defensible. Mixed tubs are recorded as "multiple" rather than guessed.
- Exact home address, serial numbers and personal account details are intentionally excluded.

### Audit scope limitation

The current conversation contains 29 image uploads and those were reviewed as a set. ChatGPT Library also exposes **more than 2,000 retained historical images**, with additional pages beyond that result window. The Library does not provide reliable semantic indexing of standalone photos, so it would be false to claim that every historical image has been manually re-opened in this pass. This register therefore combines:

1. every relevant tool/workshop photo in the current conversation;
2. prior photo-derived observations recoverable from earlier conversations;
3. relevant historical equipment observations already identified in the project history.

Treat this as the best verified baseline and extend it as older relevant photos are surfaced.

## Safety boundary

The new hobby multimeter is appropriate for **low-voltage DC output checks** and continuity. Do not use a cheap hobby meter as justification to probe live exposed 240V mains circuitry or an opened switch-mode PSU. Mains PSU internals remain outside the basic refurbishment bench boundary.
