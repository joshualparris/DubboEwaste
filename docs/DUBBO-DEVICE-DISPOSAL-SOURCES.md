## Search scope

Research combined organisation names and domains with terms including **old/retired computers, laptop refresh, IT disposal, e-waste, asset recovery, recycling, donation, remarketing, IT supplier, sustainability, annual report, procurement and tender**. Follow-up searches traced named suppliers and ownership changes rather than treating general environmental statements as device evidence.

Representative queries included:

- `MAAS Group Holdings e-waste IT disposal recycling computers`
- `Fletcher International Exports IT disposal old computers recycling`
- `Westhaven Chromebook Unitech old devices disposal`
- `Earlyrise Baking Company handheld NCS case study`
- `Dubbo RSL computer disposal annual report`
- `Inland Petroleum Reliance Technology IT`
- `Dubbo City Toyota Servco retired computers`
- `Bidfood Dubbo Midwest Foods IT disposal`
- `Western NSW LHD e-waste asset disposal supplier`
- `site:csu.edu.au "Computer Shop" "Industry Trading"`
- `TAFE NSW Greenbox Dell IT refresh remarketing`
- `Dubbo Regional Council computer disposal tender`
- `NSW government surplus laptops Device Bank WorkVentures`
- `NSW education eWaste vendor EDConnect`

This is a public-source investigation, not a complete search of private purchase orders, disposal manifests or every closed tender. “Unknown” means this research did not establish the fact. It does not mean no record or service exists.

## How to update

Retain this dated snapshot. Add a dated direct-contact or primary-document evidence record when the asset owner confirms an actual arrangement. Update the CSV's status, current provider and last-batch outcome only with that evidence ID. Public contact pages are routing sources, not proof that the person answering can authorise transfer. Keep private client information and non-public agreements out of this public report.
