# Regional ITAD Deep Research 2026 — Learning Library & 30-Day Curriculum

**Research date:** 5 October 2026  
**Purpose:** a structured watch/listen/read curriculum focused on ITAD, refurbishment, data sanitisation, reverse logistics, electronics recycling and repair economics.

**Rating convention**
- **Primary/standard:** regulator, standard, government or official technical source
- **Operator/practical:** practitioner/interview/facility perspective
- **Vendor:** technically useful but commercially interested
- **Industry:** trade association or industry podcast

### How to use YouTube

For each practical video record:
- date watched;
- publisher;
- model/tool/version;
- what procedure was demonstrated;
- what remains unverified;
- whether the advice conflicts with Australian rules.

Never convert one YouTube demonstration directly into an SOP without manufacturer/standard/regulatory verification.

# 4. Thirty-day learning plan

Assume 30–60 minutes on weekdays plus one longer session each week.

## Week 1 — Understand the business

**Day 1:** Executive synthesis + WorkVentures process  
**Day 2:** Fliptech podcast  
**Day 3:** SustainIT + Renew IT models  
**Day 4:** PonyUp + TechCollect: reuse vs recycling  
**Day 5:** write your own one-page service scope  
**Weekend:** map one fictional batch through AssetFlow end-to-end

Output:
- accepted categories;
- refused categories;
- value proposition.

## Week 2 — Data and custody

**Day 6:** NIST SP800-88r2 summary  
**Day 7:** ASD media guidance  
**Day 8:** OAIC APP11  
**Day 9:** R2 Guru sanitisation episode  
**Day 10:** compare nwipe, Blancco, Parted Magic  
**Weekend:** run synthetic wipe/evidence tests on non-sensitive test media

Output:
- sanitisation decision tree;
- release-authorisation rule;
- evidence checklist.

## Week 3 — Refurb and economics

**Day 11:** PC Right to Repair report highlights  
**Day 12:** iFixit business-laptop repairability  
**Day 13:** Lenovo HMM for a device actually in stock  
**Day 14:** All Things Circular refurbishment episode  
**Day 15:** create repair cap and labour-time sheet  
**Weekend:** process 2–3 low-value test devices with timed steps

Output:
- skill gaps;
- repair time data;
- parts list.

## Week 4 — Compliance, sourcing and growth

**Day 16:** NSW second-hand dealer rules  
**Day 17:** ACCC consumer guarantees + repair notice  
**Day 18:** SafeWork/FRNSW battery guidance  
**Day 19:** NSW public-school disposal procedure  
**Day 20:** Recycly/RazorERP comparison  
**Day 21:** write MSP pitch  
**Day 22:** write computer-shop pitch  
**Day 23:** write independent-school/business discovery script  
**Day 24:** test AssetFlow WIP/backlog controls  
**Day 25:** review downstream routes  
**Weekend:** 90-day go/no-go review.

Output:
- written regulator questions;
- source outreach list;
- next 30-device plan.

# 5. What to listen/read later, not first

Defer until basic pilot works:
- highly specialised GPU board repair;
- BGA rework;
- enterprise RFID;
- complex ERP integrations;
- advanced commodity trading;
- international resale/tax;
- full R2/e-Stewards certification implementation.

These are useful only after the simpler operating model is proven.

---

# 6. Quality rule for ongoing learning

When a new podcast/video suggests a technique:

1. classify source: regulator / standard / operator / vendor / anecdote;
2. record date/version;
3. verify against Australian context;
4. test on synthetic/low-value stock;
5. measure outcome;
6. only then convert to AssetFlow rule or SOP.

That prevents “podcast advice” from silently becoming policy.
