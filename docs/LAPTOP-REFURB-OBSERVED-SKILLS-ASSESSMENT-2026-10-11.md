# Observed practical assessment: laptop refurbishment

Updated 11 October 2026. This documents the implemented course review procedure; **no student automatically passes**.

## What was missing

Existing database reviewers could approve submissions after checking only a free-text description and a checkbox marked “directly observed or independently checked”. That can describe review of writing, not necessarily physically observing a repair. The catalogue quality report cannot tell whether an independent human has assessed a learner.

## Six observable abilities

A supervisor independently watches an authorised, supervised repair or a designated safe training-device demonstration. They score each category **0 not demonstrated; 1 partially; 2 competent with limited prompting; 3 reliable and explained**.

1. **Permission and custody**: explicitly checks owner authority, data transfer/retention plan and device identity; stops before accessing unknown data.
2. **Diagnosis and decision**: records symptoms, two alternatives, appropriate tests and a falsifiable conclusion.
3. **Compatibility and plan**: checks exact device service guide, SATA and caddy dimensions, cloning capacity and original drive preservation.
4. **Safe handling**: demonstrates battery/energy isolation, ESD precautions, correct source/target selection and recognised stop-work triggers.
5. **Verification**: repeats cold boots/restarts, checks drive model, and verifies restored files or transfer under consent.
6. **Handover**: communicates limitations, a fair itemised quote, backed-up files and documented old-drive custody.

## Approval gates

- The assessor is an authorised programme administrator **who is not the learner**, and personally observes the practical (in person or during a genuine live remote demonstration).
- Record observation date, mode, safe **redacted evidence reference** (example: `DEW-TRAINING-2026-0042`), six scores and specific written feedback.
- Total **at least 15 out of 18**, consent at least 2, safety at least 2, all six categories scored; explicit assessor attestation is required.
- An insufficient result receives **changes requested**, followed by a new learner submission after further practice.
- Existing LMS “approved” does not grant a professional licence or operating authorisation; tasks such as mains work remain separately regulated.
- Reviews cannot be silently edited once filed. Corrections require an additional submission with a traceable new review.

## Evidence privacy

Never type client names, serial numbers, disk contents, passwords, BitLocker keys or raw photos into a public repository. Use the private job/training ID in the sign-off and retain the actual authorised bench evidence in a restricted operational system per policy.

## Lifecycle

Learner enrols → completes orientation → submits a redacted reflection → authorised supervisor directly watches appropriate physical skills → supervisor records all rubric fields → database validates approval gates → learner sees observed date, evidence reference, score and status in their private LMS course.

A learner who has never submitted or been observed **remains “No independent skills sign-off recorded”.** The quality stage of the course content remains separate from the learner's assessed competence.
