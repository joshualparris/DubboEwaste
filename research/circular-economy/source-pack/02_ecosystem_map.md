## Strong repair economies "without calling it circular economy" (INFERENCE, to validate)
1. **Automotive / tyres / used parts** — repair retains high economic value.
2. **Agricultural machinery** — high capital value, long life, rural dependence on repairers.
3. **Scrap metal** — price-driven recovery.
These are the sectors where repair beats replacement. Consumer electronics and cheap appliances are where it usually does not.

## What a Repair Café can learn from them (INFERENCE)
- Repair survives where labour cost < replacement cost *and* parts are obtainable.
- Trust/reputation and local parts networks matter more than policy.
- Use scrap/wrecker relationships as a parts-donor supply for volunteers.
