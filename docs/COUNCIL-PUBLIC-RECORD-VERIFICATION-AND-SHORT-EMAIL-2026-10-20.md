## Critical questions to STOP sending as open-ended requests

- "Does Council have procurement/risk/privacy policies?" — yes (but 2026 risk update not posted under public policy URL).
- "Does any Council strategy encourage reuse?" — yes, adopted Action 6.11, 6.13.
- "Does an environmental regulator generally allow electronics reuse?" — Mark already says NSW EPA law does not appear to prohibit it; narrow legal analysis depends on site, material, processing and volumes.
- "Must it go to a formal Council meeting?" — **do not guess either way**; there is a published delegation instrument and the answer depends on legal character and value.
- "Could I simply collect a few computers from the tip?" — **no approval has been given**; numerical caps cannot create authority.
- "Is AMR definitely the Council contractor?" — **NOT established**; it may be a recycler or associated downstream player, and the historic Table 12 identifies Matthews Metals in a period already expired.
- "What precise insurance limits does Council require?" — **not publicly substantiated**; appropriate to ask once project is classified. No arbitrary $10m/$20m claims.

## ONLY THREE QUESTIONS worth asking the Waste Coordinator now

1. **Contract viability:** Does the Council's current e-waste arrangement exclude diversion of screened reusable laptops/desktops, or is there in principle a lawful Council/contractor-approved route? A yes/no/unknown with contact/referral is enough.
2. **Decision pathway:** If potentially possible, who should classify and manage such a small, donation-first proposed pilot and advise on approval/agreement pathway? Request the **correct officer**, not a list of five departments.
3. **Project-specific conditions:** Is there a particular Council checklist, minimum insurance requirement or agreement/risk template that that officer wants, **beyond publicly available policy documents**? Offer to receive it, rather than asking 20 sub-questions.

If answer to #1 is impossible under current agreement, pause direct Council proposal and focus on independent donor model with its own approvals (Fair Trading/licence, site/lease/planning, insurer and data controls). Do **not** presume any noncommercial donation model is automatically exempt from product safety, property-title or second-hand dealer obligations.
