# Core findings

## Supported

- Start narrow with useful business IT rather than advertising general e-waste.
- Prioritise reuse/refurbishment but maintain honest downstream recycling.
- Route quickly and cap WIP.
- Data sanitisation is a core service/evidence problem.
- Modular repair is enough to start.
- Computer/MSP partnerships are strategically logical.
- Purpose-built ITAD ERP is expensive enough that a lean internal system can make sense.
- Specialist/downstream capability can remain networked rather than local.

## Corrected

### “Never pay for equipment”
Too absolute.

Better:
**Do not pay before you can value.**

Mature Australian ITAD operators use buyback/rebate models.

### “DBAN is basically the wiping model”
Outdated.

Modern media requires media-aware methods, validation and evidence under the chosen policy/standard.

### “Schools are an easy source”
Not universally.

NSW public schools have a departmental e-waste contract.

### “Soldering needs a huge lab”
Not for basic work.

Advanced board/BGA work can become equipment- and skill-intensive, but normal business-device refurbishment can be done largely through modular service.

### “Gaming hardware needs deeper repair”
Plausible operator experience, but not strong enough evidence for a policy. Measure it.

# Recommended Phase 0 model

**Positioning**

> Controlled regional IT asset recovery and refurbishment for selected computers, with documented custody, secure data handling, reuse-first routing and verified downstream pathways.

**Accept first**
- business laptops;
- business desktops/mini PCs;
- associated chargers/docks;
- selected monitors/network gear with a known route.

**Defer**
- general household e-waste;
- damaged lithium;
- whitegoods;
- TVs/printers as a default lane;
- large unknown mixed loads;
- deep board repair.

**Pilot intake**
4–6 serialized data-bearing devices/week initially.

**Physical pilot caps**
- intake 2;
- unwiped 4;
- repair 3;
- QA 2;
- sale-ready 4;
- total serialized held 12.

**Backlog rule**
If inbound exceeds closed/outbound for two consecutive operating weeks, reduce or pause intake.
