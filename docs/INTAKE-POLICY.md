# Intake policy — Phase 0

**Status:** draft for the first 20–30 item pilot.  
**Model:** selective electronics reuse/refurbishment, not general waste collection.  
**Intake days:** Tuesday and Saturday only, **pre-approval required**.  
**Public walk-in intake starts only after Council, Fair Trading, tenancy/landlord and insurance questions are resolved.**

See [PHASE-0-OPERATING-BLUEPRINT.md](PHASE-0-OPERATING-BLUEPRINT.md) for the research behind these rules.

## Core rule

> **If we cannot identify a realistic reuse, repair, parts or lawful downstream route before an item arrives, we do not accept it.**

No unattended dumping. No “leave it at the gate”. Rejected items leave with the person who brought them.

## Pre-screen before arrival

Ask for:

- category
- make/model
- photos
- does it power on?
- known fault
- screen/panel damage
- battery damage/swelling/water/fire exposure
- account/activation-lock status
- charger/accessories
- quantity
- source/ownership
- whether it contains business/customer data

Respond with:

- ACCEPT
- ACCEPT SUBJECT TO INSPECTION
- REJECT
- NEED MORE INFORMATION

## Priority accepts

### Laptops / desktops / mini PCs

Prefer:

- business-class laptops
- quality consumer laptops
- MacBooks with Activation Lock removed
- mini PCs/desktops
- machines with useful SSD/RAM/parts even if repairable

**2026 default retail target:** Windows 11-compatible hardware. Windows 10 normal support ended 14 October 2025. Older PCs can still be accepted where there is a credible Linux, vintage, donor-parts or other value path.

### Phones

Accept only when:

- battery is undamaged
- device is not fire/water damaged
- Apple/Google/manufacturer locks can be legitimately removed
- IMEI/serial can be recorded
- current Australian network/000 compatibility can be verified before resale
- condition/value justify handling

3G-only and some older 4G devices are no longer viable on Australian networks after the 3G shutdown.

### Tablets

Same lock/battery rules as phones. Tablets require their own downstream route; ordinary MobileMuster collections do not generally accept them.

### TVs

**By prior approval only.**

Prefer:

- flat-panel only
- identifiable model
- intact panel
- working or plausibly economic to repair
- storage location already available

### Other good electronics

By approval where there is a known market:

- consoles
- cameras
- quality audio
- vintage computing
- networking equipment
- useful PC components

## Refuse at Phase 0

- CRT TVs/monitors
- cracked-panel TVs unless a specific safe/profitable parts route is already identified
- loose lithium batteries
- swollen/bulging/leaking/punctured/crushed/overheated batteries
- fire-affected battery devices
- water-submerged battery devices
- e-bike/e-scooter batteries
- vapes/e-cigarettes
- whitegoods
- printers/scanners unless pre-approved
- fluorescent lamps
- smoke alarms
- random mixed cable boxes
- contaminated/pest-infested electronics
- no-name/unsafe chargers and power supplies
- Apple devices with unresolved Activation Lock
- Android devices that cannot be legitimately cleared of account/device protection
- organisation-managed devices without authority to transfer
- suspicious provenance or removed/defaced serials without credible explanation
- bulk dead low-value electronics
- anything with no identified exit route

## Four acceptance gates

An accepted device must pass the applicable gates:

1. **Ownership/legal** — credible authority to transfer.
2. **Safety** — no unacceptable battery/electrical/contamination hazard.
3. **Value/technical** — resale, repair, donor, collector or known recycling route.
4. **Storage** — a labelled shelf/bin position exists before acceptance.

**Shelf full = intake paused for that category.**

## Intake record

Every accepted item receives a unique ID, for example **DEW-2026-0001**.

Record:

- date
- source
- transfer type
- make/model
- serial
- IMEI where relevant
- condition
- battery condition
- charger/accessories
- lock/account state
- data-bearing status
- known faults
- ownership/authority statement
- initial route
- photos

If Fair Trading confirms a second-hand dealer licence is required, this record must be expanded to the prescribed statutory fields/reporting process.

## Data workflow

Every data-bearing device goes immediately to:

**UNWIPED — RESTRICTED**

Workflow:

1. identify/record media
2. remove legitimate account/activation locks at handover where possible
3. store unwiped devices securely
4. sanitise using media-appropriate method
5. verify result
6. record method/date/operator/result
7. mark **SANITISED**
8. only then move to repair, parts or resale

Do not retain customer Apple/Google passwords.

Marketing language at Phase 0:

> **Documented secure data erasure**

Do not advertise “certified data destruction” unless a later process/accreditation genuinely supports that claim.

## Battery rule

Phase 0 **does not operate a battery drop-off service**.

- visually inspect battery-bearing devices before acceptance
- reject damaged/swollen/wet/fire-affected battery devices
- never use or charge a damaged battery
- no unattended or overnight charging
- use compatible/compliant chargers
- stop testing if abnormal heat, smell, swelling or noise occurs
- use an accredited/authorised downstream route for battery waste

Do **not** place badly damaged/swollen batteries into an ordinary B-cycle drop point.

## Resale

Second-hand goods sold by a business remain subject to Australian Consumer Law.

For every sale:

- give accurate make/model/spec
- use consistent condition grading
- show clear photos
- disclose known faults
- identify included charger/accessories
- retain serial/asset ID
- keep test record
- issue receipt/invoice as required
- keep buyer/contact records required by law and enough to act on later safety issues

Do not use “no refunds” or “sold as-is” wording to imply statutory consumer rights do not apply.

## Storage zones

Use clearly labelled areas:

1. INTAKE / UNASSESSED
2. UNWIPED — RESTRICTED
3. TEST / REPAIR
4. READY FOR SALE
5. DONOR / PARTS
6. OUTBOUND RECYCLING
7. QUARANTINE / STOP

Rules:

- no outdoor stock
- no stock on damp floors
- no stock against damp/mould-affected surfaces
- no device without an asset ID
- no miscellaneous pile
- each zone has a physical capacity
- zone full = pause intake

**The existing shed must be demonstrated dry, secure and suitable before storing valuable or unwiped electronics there.**

## Downstream

Before public intake, confirm:

- Australian Metal Recycling Dubbo categories/fees/lithium rules **and the named downstream recycler**
- NTCRS option for TVs/computers/peripherals
- MobileMuster option for dead phones/chargers/accessories
- safe damaged-battery pathway
- whether AMR can provide a weight ticket/certificate of recycling for small commercial loads
- whether any downstream facility in the AMR chain is AS 5377 certified

**Data-bearing rule:** do not rely on ordinary AMR recycling as evidence of data destruction. Sanitise media first or remove HDD/SSD/NVMe storage for a separately verified destruction path.

**Battery rule:** AMR's site simultaneously lists laptops/phones/tablets and excludes lithium-ion material. Do not send battery-equipped devices to AMR until the Dubbo yard confirms exactly how that rule works.

See [WHAT-HAPPENS-TO-EWASTE.md](WHAT-HAPPENS-TO-EWASTE.md) for the detailed downstream trace.

Do not treat the household CRC as an assumed commercial waste-disposal service.
