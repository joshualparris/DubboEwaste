## Publicly advertised features

CRM and quote versions; e-signatures; collections and driver allocation; barcode receiving; multiwarehouse stock; carrier rates and labels; Xero/QuickBooks payment sync; erasure integrations; versioned emissions factors; customer dashboards; ecommerce; timesheets; knowledgebase; JSON-RPC and webhooks.

## Limits and unresolved details

UK waste reporting is jurisdiction-specific. Confirm adapter formats, Australian carriers and accounting tax behaviour.
