# Downstream provider comparison: Dubbo E-Waste

**Checked:** 2 October 2026  
**Question:** Which of the shortlisted organisations can plausibly service Dubbo, NSW, and what would Dubbo E-Waste pay?  
**Evidence rule:** “Confirmed” means the organisation’s current public material explicitly supports the claim. “Likely” means the organisation advertises national/regional logistics but does not name Dubbo. “Unknown” requires a phone call or written quote.

## Short answer

The strongest immediate lead is **Ecoactiv**, which explicitly lists Dubbo among its regional NSW collection areas. For quality devices and social reuse, contact **WorkVentures/CircularIT** and the **National Device Bank**. For end-of-life mixed loads, request a quote from **Sircel**, whose network includes regional NSW and a Parkes facility. Use **MobileMuster** for phones and accessories, and use **Ecycle Solutions/TechCollect** for eligible free recycling routes after confirming the current Dubbo drop-off or pickup arrangement.

No provider in this list publishes a complete Dubbo commercial rate card. “Free” normally means the service is funded by the NTCRS, a product-stewardship program, a council, a manufacturer, or a cross-subsidy—not that the underlying transport, sorting, data-wiping and processing costs disappear.

## What “cost” means for the pilot

There are four different commercial outcomes and they should not be mixed in the pilot tracker:

1. **Free stewardship route:** Ecoactiv, Ecycle, TechCollect and MobileMuster may be free when the item and quantity fit their funded program.
2. **Quote-only disposal:** Sircel, WorkVentures, Renew IT, Fliptech, SK tes and PonyUp may charge, waive, or rebate depending on volume, condition, data requirements and freight.
3. **Social-reuse contribution:** Good360’s published $100/$150/$240 figures describe the cost of testing, wiping, refurbishing, delivery, data and impact—not necessarily a fee for someone handing over a broken laptop.
4. **Value recovery:** WorkVentures, Renew IT, PonyUp and Sircel may return value for suitable assets. That is a separate number from the cost of processing the rejects.

## Quote request to send each commercial provider

Ask for a written response using the same facts:

- Dubbo NSW 2830 pickup address and whether the site is accessible to a rigid truck;
- estimated monthly volume by category: laptops, desktops, monitors, phones, TVs, printers, networking gear and loose parts;
- approximate weights and pallet/cage count;
- percentage believed working, repairable, locked, data-bearing, damaged or battery-affected;
- whether Dubbo E-Waste has already triaged and serialised the equipment;
- required data standard and whether certificates are per device;
- pickup, freight, pallet/cage, sorting, battery and non-conforming-item fees;
- minimum load and collection frequency;
- reuse, resale, donation and recycling split;
- rebate/buyback formula for reusable equipment;
- reporting: weight, asset list, chain of custody, wipe certificates and final route;
- GST, insurance, dangerous-goods conditions and cancellation fees.

## Recommended first calls

1. **Ecoactiv:** confirm a free Dubbo NTCRS pickup for a defined pilot batch.
2. **Sircel:** request a quote for mixed end-of-life material plus separate reuse/ITAD treatment.
3. **WorkVentures/CircularIT:** ask whether good laptops and phones can enter the National Device Bank/social-reuse pathway.
4. **National Device Bank:** ask whether Dubbo E-Waste can aggregate more than 50 qualifying devices or act as a regional collection partner.
5. **MobileMuster:** establish a separate phone/accessory stream with a collection unit.
6. **Ecycle Solutions and TechCollect:** confirm the nearest current Dubbo route for free NTCRS material.

This research does not establish that any provider has agreed to service Dubbo, nor does it replace a written quote, waste-licence check, dangerous-goods check or data-processing agreement.
