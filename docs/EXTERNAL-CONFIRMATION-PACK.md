## A. NSW Fair Trading — second-hand dealer / exemption

**Subject:** Written guidance request — small electronics reuse/refurbishment model

Proposed facts to state:
- small Dubbo operation;
- appointment/pre-approved intake only;
- laptops, phones, tablets and related electronics;
- items accepted free or purchased depending source;
- first priority is reuse/refurbishment/resale;
- non-viable items sent to legitimate recycling;
- serial/IMEI/source records retained;
- data-bearing items kept restricted until erased;
- no public unattended waste bin.

Questions:
1. Does this mixed reuse/refurbish/recycle model fall within the exemption for “recycling and rubbish collection programs”?
2. If a second-hand dealer licence is required, which activities are prohibited during the 14-day retention period?
3. Does secure data erasure count as “altering” goods?
4. Can diagnostic testing occur during the hold if it does not physically alter the item?
5. Can Fair Trading grant a limited exemption allowing data erasure while preserving the item and full serial/provenance records?
6. What evidence/conditions should accompany an exemption request?
7. Is there a fee for an exemption application?
8. For business-fleet assets transferred under written authority, do the same hold rules apply?
