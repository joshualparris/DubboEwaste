# Launch Scenarios

## Scenario A — known-source pilot
**Sources:** own/known contacts and clearly authorised business assets.  
**Public intake:** no.  
**Advantages:** lowest stolen-goods, traffic and dumping risk; best for learning.  
**Weakness:** low volume.  
**Use:** preferred first 30 devices while regulatory answers are pending, only where lawful.

## Scenario B — appointment public intake
**Sources:** pre-screened residents/businesses.  
**Public bin:** none.  
**Advantages:** more supply; local visibility.  
**Risks:** planning, second-hand dealer, insurance, unsafe/random junk, storage.  
**Gate:** written Council/Fair Trading + insurance first.

## Scenario C — MSP/school fleet collection
**Sources:** repeat organisations.  
**Advantages:** known provenance, repeat models, batch efficiency, better reporting value.  
**Risks:** conflict-of-interest, client data, procurement expectations, MDM release, professional liability.  
**Gate:** employment conflict resolved + chain-of-custody/sanitisation process proven.

## Comparison
| Dimension | A Known-source | B Public appointment | C Fleet/MSP |
|---|---|---|---|
| supply | low | medium | medium/high |
| provenance | high | variable | high |
| legal complexity | lower | highest | medium/high |
| marketing need | low | medium | relationship |
| batch efficiency | low | low | high |
| recommended sequence | **1** | 3 | **2 after conflict/process proof** |
