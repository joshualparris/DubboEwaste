# Commercial terms / quote request

Use the same questions across downstream processors, ITAD partners, sanitisation providers and insurers where relevant.

Please confirm:

1. minimum number of devices;
2. minimum weight;
3. minimum pickup value/threshold;
4. Dubbo pickup availability/frequency;
5. pickup/transport fee;
6. per-unit fee;
7. per-kg fee or rebate;
8. buyback/residual-value method;
9. accepted/rejected categories;
10. lithium/embedded-battery conditions;
11. data-bearing-device requirements;
12. sanitisation/destruction method and standard;
13. certificate/report supplied;
14. whether reports are serialised;
15. turnaround time;
16. minimum contract term/account requirement;
17. insurance/certification requirements for a regional subcontractor;
18. whether the provider would accept a small Dubbo aggregation/refurbishment partner;
19. whether reusable devices may be diverted before recycling; and
20. how long the quote/terms remain valid.

Ask for a written quote, service schedule, sample report/certificate, terms/exclusions and relevant accreditation evidence.

Record privately in **AssetFlow /validation → Commercial/private terms**.
