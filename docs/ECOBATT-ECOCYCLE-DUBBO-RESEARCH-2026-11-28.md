# EcoBatt / EcoCycle: Dubbo electronic-waste supply-chain investigation
28 November 2026 | Public-source research | AUD | No partnership, access or disposal authority granted.

## Executive verdict
EcoBatt and ecoe-waste are registered business names of ECOCYCLE PTY LTD, ABN 76 146 190 516 [1], not necessarily separate legal subsidiaries. EcoCycle is an approved NTCRS co-regulatory arrangement [2]. Its secure-disposal page promises physical destruction and no refurbishment/resale of e-waste [3]. This is a company policy, not proof every asset of every customer is destroyed or that all group contracts prohibit reuse. BIG W explicitly prohibits retrieval of deposited mobile devices [4]. The actionable opportunity is an owner-approved separate reuse-first intake BEFORE surrender to recycling, not salvage from existing bins. No Dubbo-to-EcoCycle shipment or corporate ITAD contract has been traced.

## 4. Why destruction, and legal gates
EcoCycle submission 217 (11 Apr 2025), p3 [35], prioritises scheme reform and audit transparency before introducing reuse targets; stresses consumer expectation of destruction. Productivity Commission final report supports stronger stewardship and repair/reuse [36]. Policy disagreement is not evidence of illegality. Distinct opt-in donation BEFORE waste acceptance is the strongest model.
- Commonwealth Recycling and Waste Reduction Act 2020, NTCRS 2021 Rules [2,37]: producer obligations and annual reporting. Government explicitly invites recycler service-provider arrangements [38]; request co-reg approval and reporting rules; no assumed reuse credit. National EPA commenced July 2026 per department page; check current administering contact.
- NSW Product Lifecycle Responsibility Act 2025 and Regulation 2026 [39]: mandatory regulated battery stewardship framework; check commencement/transitional dates, scope and NSW recycling standard. Do not accept loose/damaged lithium without trained, licensed/authorised route. NSW POEO Act, licence thresholds, planning/zoning, fire and dangerous-goods requirements need site-specific professional assessment. No grant or scrap registration is an exemption.
- AS/NZS 5377 certification scope; current edition and each scheme's required version must be confirmed. OAIC APP11 [40] requires applicable entities to secure and destroy/de-identify unneeded personal information subject to exceptions. NIST SP 800-88 Rev 2 [41] permits risk-appropriate clear/purge/destroy; not Australian legal authority or blanket permission to override contract. Confirm ASD ISM/customer rules for government/classified media. If sanitisation cannot be verified, quarantine and send for approved destruction; no data browsing. Obtain ownership release, asset inventory, lock removal by authorised owner, wipe validation, certificates, secure storage, insurance, safety tests and ACL obligations [42]. Check second-hand dealer requirements and licence exemptions with NSW authorities. No legal advice.
- MobileMuster accredited voluntary scheme [29]; B-cycle and NSW PSO arrangements distinct. Do not use scheme logos or claim accreditation without approval. No present evidence of EcoCycle reuse exception or component-sale permission. Ask for separate service agreement, not change to destruction-only contract.

## 5. Commercial model: illustrative, not actual volumes
DCCEEW 2019 national estimate 511,000t (~20kg/person) [43]. For an illustrative 55,000-person catchment, 20kg/person implies 1,100t/year ALL e-waste, not IT-only, actual Dubbo generation or EcoCycle capture. Population assumption not verified. Council's total waste tonnage is not e-waste. No local device counts, collection frequency, recoverable share, avoided CO2 factors or disposal rates established.
Model per incoming consented device: $15 intake/testing/logistics (0.25h labour at $40 + $5 other); successful device additional $70 (1h labour $40, parts $25, warranty/admin $5), sale $150; rejected device $5 downstream fee. No acquisition price, lease, rent, insurance, GST, transport quotes, donations, or fixed overhead in this illustrative model. Contribution = N*(p*150 - 15 - p*70 - (1-p)*5) = N*(85p-20). Low yield 15%, base 35%, high 60%. Break-even 23.53% before fixed costs. If donated instead of sold, budget sponsor funding; never count same unit as sale and donation.

Base 100: 35 successful, 65 rejects, $5,250 revenue, $4,275 variable cost, $975 contribution. 100 devices at 0.25h intake plus 35h repairs = 60 hours labour. At 5,000 base, 3,000 hours (not one person's spare-time project). A $65 locked-phone service cost on each recovered phone can remove most margin; exclude locked devices unless owner or approved route resolves economically. No assumption about recoverability or actual price. Track weighed reuse and residuals; avoided emissions only after evidence-based life-cycle comparison, displacement and lifetime assumptions.

## 6. Recommended pilot and evidence gates
A. Approach EcoCycle partnerships and local asset owners for separately labelled opt-in donation/reuse stream (not existing kiosk). B. Written agreement specifies title transfer, exclusions, data destruction requests remain untouched, owner consent, programme reporting, insurance, quarantine, safe transport, downstream rejects. C. 100-unit capped intake over 90 days only after approval, preferably desktop PCs and owner-unlocked laptops; no hazardous batteries or recalled goods. D. Inventory serials privately, quarantine/triage, certified sanitisation per owner agreement, test condition and electrical safety, record outcomes and sell/donate with disclosures. E. Send rejects via approved recycler with weights/certificates. F. Review before scaling: 100% chain-of-custody, 100% validated sanitisation or destruction, zero unsafe release, tracked recovery %, labour, cost, warranty returns, and no double-counting scheme tonnage. Stop if safety/custody failures or poor economics. Alternative models: local corporate ITAD/reuse (high priority), collection subcontracting (requires tender and compliant logistics; does not confer reuse rights), pre-consent donation (high), post-bin diversion (not recommended), component harvest only by explicit permission, Council pilot subject to procurement.
