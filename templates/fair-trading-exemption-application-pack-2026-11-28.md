# Exemption application pack (for use only after licensing): DRAFT, 28 November 2026

**Status: DRAFT, NOT LODGED.**

**Why this exists:** Act s 4A lets the Secretary exempt a person, by written notice, from the whole Act or specified provisions, with conditions and an expiry. Details of each exemption are published ([research §2 and §8](../docs/RECYCLING-PROGRAM-EXEMPTION-RESEARCH-2026-11-28.md)).

Every exemption published so far (list as of 27 February 2025) went to a **licensee**. Each lifts specific duties: ID evidence (s 15), records (s 16) and the 14-day retention (s 21). None removes the licence itself.

So there are two possible uses:
- **Main use (after licensing):** seek relief from rules that clash with safe refurbishment, chiefly the 14-day hold with no alteration.
- **Long shot (before licensing):** ask whether a small, serial-tracked, free-collection program could be exempted from s 7. There's no precedent for this; ask Fair Trading first (enquiry question 9).

**Lodgement:** Fair Trading's licensing exemptions address, recorded in [LEGAL-LICENSING.md](../docs/LEGAL-LICENSING.md). Application cost and processing time weren't published on the pages read. Ask first.

## Precedents to cite (from the published list)

| Licensee | What was exempted | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Alegre Pty Ltd (2PS11940) | s 15(1),(1A) ID; s 16(1) electronic records; s 21(1) retention | Track each device by serial or IMEI; records producible in English; seller held goods for the required period |
| Asurion Australia (2PS16175) | s 16(5A), s 21(1) for IMEI/serial-tracked phones, laptops, tablets, wearables | Notify changes to business processes |
| TES-AMM Australia (2PS04726) | s 16(5A) furnishing records to police | Notify if buying directly from individuals or small corporations |

All three are large operators with structured supply. A small regional program would need to show equivalent controls.

## What to request

- **Primary:** relief from the s 21(1) no-alteration period for devices received free from identified businesses or households, where each device is serial or IMEI tracked and checked before work starts. The purpose is to allow prompt data erasure, which protects donors.
- **Optional:** confirmation of the records software or format that satisfies s 16 for low-volume donated stock.

## Evidence to attach

1. Licence number and notified premises.
2. A description of supply: who gives devices, typical monthly volume, and the share that is free versus bought.
3. A sample AssetFlow record showing serial or IMEI, donor transfer, and the lost-or-stolen check result.
4. The data-sanitisation procedure and a sample certificate ([data-sanitisation-certificate.md](data-sanitisation-certificate.md)).
5. The donor transfer form ([asset-transfer-and-chain-of-custody.md](asset-transfer-and-chain-of-custody.md)).
6. The procedure for suspected stolen goods: hold, notify police, and no work on the item.
7. An offer of conditions: notify Fair Trading if buying from individuals starts, or if supply changes.

## Don'ts

- Don't lead with a blanket exemption from the licence. s 4A allows one in law, but none has been published. A narrow request with tracking conditions is more credible.
- Don't describe controls that aren't actually in place.
