# Major Australian e-waste, ITAD, reuse and device-redistribution players

**Research date:** 2 October 2026

**Purpose:** market map for DubboEwaste. This is a practical list of major national operators, important regional operators, stewardship schemes, reuse/social-enterprise organisations and collection channels relevant to e-waste, IT asset disposition (ITAD), refurbishment, data destruction, resale, donation and downstream recycling.

> This is **not a strict size ranking**. Private operators do not publish directly comparable national volumes. "Major" here means nationally significant, government-recognised, large-scale, strategically relevant, or particularly useful as a benchmark/partner for DubboEwaste.

## 8. What not to treat as equivalent competitors

These organisations occupy different parts of the chain:

- **ITAD operators**: secure enterprise collection, asset tracking, sanitisation, refurbishment, remarketing and recycling.
- **Material recyclers**: physical processing and commodity recovery.
- **Co-regulatory arrangements**: administer NTCRS obligations and contract collection/recycling services.
- **Retail collection channels**: collect devices and pass them into downstream networks.
- **Digital-inclusion organisations**: source, refurbish and redistribute usable devices to people who need them.
- **Battery schemes/processors**: handle a hazardous and increasingly important e-waste sub-stream.

DubboEwaste should therefore benchmark each organisation for the specific capability it represents instead of treating all of them as direct competitors.

## 10. Research cautions

- Do not infer actual processing volumes, profit, client lists, downstream routes or regional willingness from marketing claims alone.
- NTCRS co-regulatory status is a regulatory fact and should be checked against the current government list before relying on it.
- Several names overlap organisationally: **TechCollect is operated by ANZRP**, **Ecoactiv is part of Activ Group**, and **SPS has links to TES**. They should not be double-counted when estimating market concentration.
- A collection point or program is not necessarily the organisation physically processing the material.
